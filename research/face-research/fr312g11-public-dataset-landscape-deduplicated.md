## Priority and division of responsibility

1. **New, no raw files yet — HSRD-100:** inspect **metadata-only** dataset structure, person/pose manifests, per-mesh face-region and neutral-configuration documentation; confirm whether participant releases cover prospective commercial geometry model development. At just **10 human subjects**, even perfect meshes cannot justify a reliable participant-clustered variance/sizing estimate without separately reviewed data. High disk footprint (~246GB repository) makes indiscriminate archive download irrational.
2. **New, no raw files yet — FairFace:** inspect authoritative image/source-rights provenance and crop/landmark definitions; it can potentially expose 2D image robustness issues but **cannot** supply FR312F 2-session/2-fresh-neutral-capture data from its published dataset structure.
3. **Rights blocker — EURECOM:** document the missing second neutral capture per session and commercial applicant limitations; a generic 'two sessions' headline cannot be treated as an 8-axis reliability dataset.
4. **Already owned by `face-engine`:** AST, RAP3DF, MINDS-Libras, UL-DD, FR299 3D metric physical-unit provenance. Do not open competing issues, rerun depth experiments, submit duplicate requests or override their gates.
5. **Already owned by `face-research`:** CMU and FRGC legal inquiries FR312G10. Await actual owner responses, do not assume licences are signed.

### Independent rights and scientific-admission rules

- Dataset's published CC BY 4.0 claim concerns the publisher's licensable material; this does not by itself certify every person granted commercial biometric measurement consent, secondary processing, sensitive inferences, cloud transfers, or jurisdiction-specific privacy sufficiency. Absence of express permission ≠ proof of prohibition; classify 'unverified' rather than inventing an owner denial.
- A person is not 10 independent people because of 10 poses, 12 sessions, 100 video frames or 20 expressions. For FR312F, the target remains **two temporally distinct sessions × two fresh accepted neutral captures per session**, with development only, independently admissible per-axis measurements, unavailable reasons and participant-level uncertainty.
- Eight FR312G ratio axes (central groove length/corridor, mouth aspect/relative mesh span, signed mouth corner, upper/lower/combined visible lip bands) have **no** dataset-specific demonstrated exact matching algorithms and participant-clustered repeated measurements. No 2D landmark count, clinical face-distance ICC or 3D model count is substituted.
- This matrix is **public descriptive evidence only**. FR312G6 approved numeric evidence **0/8**, participant count/partition numeric values remain null; FR312H, traditional meaning claims, biometric collection, production and commerce authorizations remain false.
- No external images, biometric data or participant records acquired here. No new provider email sent, cost incurred, participant recruitment, sample-size estimate or product activation.
