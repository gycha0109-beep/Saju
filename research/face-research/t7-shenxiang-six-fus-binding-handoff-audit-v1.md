## 4. Descriptor audit — 9/9

No T5 descriptor receives a traditional binding.

The six Context A descriptor units remain blocked because they combine unresolved traditional regions, unresolved compound constructs, missing capture-controlled appearance authority, or missing ear/hair relation evidence.

Existing geometry does not authorize substitutions such as `cheek width = 方正`, `cheek contour prominence = 綳鼓`, `bilateral asymmetry = 高低`, `lower-face width = 粗/大`, or `visible contour = 尖削`.

Appearance-sensitive wording such as 明淨, 昏, and 慘 is also blocked. FR282 is an ordinary-RGB morphology authority, not a calibrated traditional appearance contract. Raw brightness, RGB values, or skin tone therefore cannot be promoted into those constructs.

The three Context B aggregate units — `充實相輔`, `不欲支離孤露`, and `六府充直，無缺陷瘢痕` — remain unresolved. The audit rejects global symmetry→相輔, 3D volume→充實, segmentation gaps→支離, depth peak→孤露, straightness metric→充直, and medical scar model→瘢痕 shortcuts.
