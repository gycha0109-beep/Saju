## 1. Purpose

T4 reconstructed the source-qualified 柳莊 methodology.

T5 asks:

> What neutral observations, capture conditions, and traditional bindings would be required before any of those source constructs could become executable?

This phase specifies contracts. It does not implement CV bindings.

## 3. Fail-closed block classes

| Block class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| observation contract + binding | neutral geometry is plausible but traditional construct validity is absent |
| capture protocol + binding | appearance/exposure depends on controlled capture |
| multi-state protocol + binding | one image cannot supply the source relation |
| source-grounded figurative definition | historical analogy is not a numeric shape metric |
| semantic region definition | traditional named region is not yet authorized |
| compound construct definition | source phrase cannot be safely decomposed into executable parts |

A blocked criterion is not a negative reading. It is **not executable**.

## 4. 採聽官

- `色明`: controlled ear appearance required; raw RGB prohibited.
- `高聳過於眉`: neutral ear/brow relation is only a candidate until reference points are governed.
- `輪廓完成`: blocked on 輪/廓/完成 semantics.
- `貼肉敦厚`: neutral attachment/projection/fullness candidates do not establish the traditional compound.
- `命門寬大`: blocked first on 柳莊-specific 命門 region authority.

The existing neutral ear/mesh assets do not become `命門` automatically.

## 5. 保壽官

- `寬廣清長`: preserve as a compound; no independent thresholds.
- `雙分入鬢`: requires governed 鬢 identity.
- `玄犀新月`: figurative-form research; curvature alone is insufficient.
- `首尾豐盈`: requires governed brow-end roles/fullness.
- `高居額中`: requires governed 額中 identity and relation.

## 6. 監察官

This slice is capture-sensitive.

- `含藏不露`: controlled gaze/expression.
- `黑白分明`: controlled illumination/exposure/white balance.
- `瞳子端正`: controlled visible iris/pupil evidence; not a diagnosis.
- `光彩射人`: camera highlights must not be equated with source 光彩.
- `鳳目細長藏秀`: figurative/compound research; not eye aspect ratio.

## 7. 審辨官

- `樑柱明直`: neutral nasal-axis evidence may be a candidate, not 樑柱 authority.
- `上接山根 / 印堂明潤`: 山根/印堂 binding and controlled appearance required.
- `下連年壽高隆`: 年壽 region authority required; 2D height does not prove 3D prominence.
- `不宜起節`: a negative source phrase does not authorize an automatic veto.
- `準頭庫起`: 準頭/庫 identities must be governed before measurement.
- `懸膽`: figurative comparanda required.
- `截筒`: figurative comparanda required.
- `黃明色肉`: controlled appearance only; no sensitive-attribute inference.

## 8. 出納官

- `角弓`: figurative-form definition required.
- `開大合小`: explicitly a same-subject controlled multi-state problem.
- `上下唇配齒配四方`: compound semantics remain unresolved.

A single selfie cannot satisfy `開大合小`.

## 9. 六府 binding boundary

柳莊 T4 preserves:

```text
天倉 → 上二府
顴骨 → 中二府
地庫 → 下二府
```

T5 may request neutral evidence near the relevant facial areas, but the observation engine must not emit `天倉`, `顴骨`, `地庫`, or 上/中/下二府 merely because geometry exists.

Traditional identity belongs to the binding layer.

The 神相 六府 region system remains separate.

## 10. Existing neutral assets

Existing neutral observation work such as FR192, FR216, FR217, FR282, FR286, MESH2, MESH6B, RAP3DF and later neutral geometry work may be useful evidence providers.

They are **not** automatically traditional bindings.

```text
neutral observation
!=
traditional region
!=
traditional criterion
```

T5 therefore records required observation capabilities rather than assigning those assets to 柳莊 labels.
