## 7. Context firewall

The following remain explicit non-equivalences.

```text
Context A 天府 != automatically Context B 上二府
Context A 人府 != automatically Context B 中二府
Context A 地府 != automatically Context B 下二府

Context A 兩顴 != automatically Context B 兩顴骨
```

A future observation engine may expose a neutral primitive that is useful to both contexts.

That technical reuse still does not establish shared traditional meaning.

Each traditional binding must be reviewed against its exact `methodologyRef`.

## 8. Why the T5 analytical facets are not source grammar

T5 decomposes compound expressions into research-planning facets where useful.

For example:

- shape/form;
- bilateral relation;
- visible prominence/exposure;
- capture-controlled surface appearance.

Those facets describe evidence that future research may need.

They do not rewrite the source into:

```text
criterion A
AND criterion B
AND criterion C
```

The same applies to positive and negative wording.

Countervailing descriptors do not become automatic vetoes.
