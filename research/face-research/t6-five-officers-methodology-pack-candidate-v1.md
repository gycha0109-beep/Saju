```text

= what evidence would be needed

= how a machine calculates something
```

### Nose

Existing neutral nose work can be useful downstream, but:

- bridge geometry is not automatically 梁柱 or 山根;
- tip/projection geometry is not automatically 準 or 庫;
- 年壽 needs its own region authority;
- 懸膽/截筒 need source-grounded figurative constructs.

- 方大 is not one aspect ratio;
- 端厚 is not thickness alone;
- 角弓 is not generic corner curvature;
- 開大合小 is multi-state;
- 唇紅 requires controlled color capture.
