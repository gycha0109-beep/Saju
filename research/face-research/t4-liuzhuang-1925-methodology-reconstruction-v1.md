### 命門

`命門寬大` belongs to this 柳莊 source formula.

### 玄犀新月

This source prints `玄犀新月`.

### 鳳目細長藏秀

The source uses a figurative/compound alternative.

### 印堂 / 山根 / 年壽 / 準頭 / 庫

Neutral CV observations may later provide evidence, but region authority belongs to the binding layer.

### 開大合小

This expression implies more than one mouth state.

A single selfie cannot establish the dynamic relation.
