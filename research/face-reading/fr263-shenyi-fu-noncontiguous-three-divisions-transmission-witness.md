# FR263 — 神異賦 Non-Contiguous Three-Divisions Transmission Witness

Watchtower-Track: face-research

## 1. Purpose

FR263 pins one exact later compilation witness for the non-contiguous face 三停 formula that FR261 keeps separate from the 1925 麻衣 contiguous 三停 lineage.

This phase does **not** claim that the compilation is the original 神異賦 manuscript.

It establishes only that a distinct transmitted 神異賦 context explicitly uses:

- 髮際 → 印堂
- 山根 → 準頭
- 人中 → 地閣

as face 三停.

## 3. Pinned passage

Lead:

`三停平等，一生衣祿無虧`

The page then defines the face 三停 as:

1. `自髮際至印堂為上停`
2. `山根至於準頭為中停`
3. `人中至地閣為下停`

The same passage also distinguishes body 三停.

This is sufficient to establish the existence of an independently transmitted non-contiguous face Three-Divisions formula.

## 4. What this does not establish

FR263 does not establish that:

- the compilation is the original 神異賦 text;
- the compilation wording is identical to every earlier 神異賦 witness;
- the 1925 麻衣 first triplet is therefore 三停;
- the non-contiguous formula overrides the 麻衣 contiguous formula;
- one formula should be universalized across lineages.

The result is lineage separation, not reconciliation.

## 5. Relationship to FR33 and FR261

FR33 historically preserved both geometries because their context was unresolved.
