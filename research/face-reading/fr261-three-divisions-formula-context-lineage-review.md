# FR261 — Three Divisions Formula Context / Lineage Review

Watchtower-Track: face-research

## 1. Purpose

FR261 reviews why FR33 preserves two different 麻衣 三停 span families and whether they should be modeled as a single winner/loser variant pair.

FR261 does **not** select a universal Three-Divisions formula.

It keeps FR33 unresolved and adds context / lineage structure around the ambiguity.

## 2. Primary repository evidence

The verified 1925 NLC 麻衣相法 witness already establishes that both formula families are genuine transmission data.

### Non-contiguous family

- 髮際 → 印堂
- 山根 → 準頭
- 人中 → 地閣

FR33 scan-checks all three clauses.

The 1925 Mayi witness uses 上停 / 中停 / 下停 wording for this family.

### Contiguous family

- 髮際 → 眉
- 眉 → 準頭
- 準頭 → 地閣

FR33 also scan-checks this family from the same witness.

Therefore neither family may be discarded as OCR noise.

## 3. Shenxiang context distinction

The repository currently contains unverified electronic transcriptions for two 神相全編 contexts:

- 面三停
- 三才三停論

Independent electronic witnesses reproduce a notable contextual distinction.

In 三才三停論, the non-contiguous family is presented with 初主 / 中主 / 末主 language and Fu-like labels, followed immediately by the contiguous family as explicit 上停 / 中停 / 下停.

In the dedicated 面三停 section, the contiguous family is again used.

This is strong contextual corroboration, but the repository's 神相全編 passage remains `unverified_ocr`.

FR261 therefore records:

`shenxiang_context_role_separation_corroborated_but_scan_admission_pending`

It does not promote the Shenxiang transcription to scan-checked authority.

## 4. Why non-contiguous ≠ globally "Fu"

The non-contiguous family cannot be globally relabeled as a Fu-only construct.

Two reasons:

1. the scan-checked 1925 Mayi witness itself calls the non-contiguous spans 三停;
2. independent transmission of the 神異賦 / 麻衣 quotation uses the same non-contiguous geometry directly under 三停平等.

Therefore:

`noncontiguous_anchor_span_family != globally_fu_only`

The formula family must be identified by its anchors and witness/context, not by one terminology label.

## 5. Why contiguous ≠ globally "the only true Three Divisions"

The contiguous family has strong explicit face-Three-Divisions support in Shenxiang transmission.

That does not erase the scan-checked Mayi non-contiguous 三停 transmission.

Therefore:

`contiguous_anchor_span_family != globally_only_true_three_divisions`

## 6. Liuzhuang

柳莊相法 remains separate.

Its repository transcription uses:

- 髮際 → 山根
- 山根 → 準頭
- 人中 → 地閣

This is neither FR33 Mayi family.

FR261 does not collapse Liuzhuang into Mayi or Shenxiang.

## 7. Repository model decision

FR261 recommends a successor model based on:

`witness-qualified formula families + context roles`

The two core formula-family identities are neutral:

- `noncontiguous_anchor_span_family`
- `contiguous_anchor_span_family`

Context roles are attached separately.

This avoids making geometry identity depend on whether a particular witness writes 府, 輔, 停, 三主, or 三停.

## 8. FR33 / FR34 / FR36 impact
