# FR258 — Face-Research Master Frontier

Watchtower-Track: face-research

## 1. Purpose

FR258 turns the current Face Reading research corpus into one fail-closed research frontier.

It audits each major traditional slice across exactly seven axes:

1. source authority
2. concept authority
3. neutral observable
4. metric
5. empirical evidence
6. metric-to-concept mapping
7. semantic claim

FR258 is an audit and sequencing artifact only. It does not promote any traditional concept, provider binding, metric binding, empirical sufficiency, threshold, criterion state, structured claim, Production authority, or Commerce authority.

## 2. Why this exists

The repository already has several strong but separate research lines:

- Five Officers / intake / 方大 source and metric research
- Eye-pair 細/長 mapping-evidence research
- Three Divisions source/anchor/derivation contracts
- Six-Fus lineage-specific maps
- Twelve-Palace lineage-specific locators
- whole-face neutral measurement inventory
- live repeat-capture and empirical evidence infrastructure

Without a master frontier, a new research phase can accidentally duplicate a live evidence lane, confuse neutral measurement readiness with traditional semantic readiness, or jump from a research locator directly to a user-facing claim.

FR258 makes those boundaries explicit.

## 4. Selected non-overlapping frontier

FR258 selects:

`three_divisions_mayi`

This is not a semantic promotion.

The selection rationale is sequencing only:

- it does not duplicate the active Fang/Da capture lane;
- it does not duplicate the Xi/Chang pre-collection lane;
- FR33/FR34 already preserve source variants and neutral-anchor requirements;
- FR35 already defines missing neutral surfaces without provider binding;
- FR36 already defines seven vertical-reference contracts;
- the concrete gap is therefore visible: reviewed/executable neutral vertical-reference derivation is missing.

The next research step must remain bounded to the smallest executable vertical-reference dependency.
