# FR262 — 麻衣 Contiguous Three-Divisions Four-Anchor Requirements Successor

Watchtower-Track: face-research

## 1. Purpose

FR262 replaces the old **Mayi successor dependency graph**, not the historical FR34 artifact.

FR261 has already adjudicated the 1925 NLC 麻衣相法 卷一 source context:

- the non-contiguous 髮際→印堂 / 山根→準頭 / 人中→地閣 triplet belongs to the 三府/三主 context in that passage;
- the following contiguous 髮際→眉 / 眉→準頭 / 準頭→地閣 triplet is the 麻衣 Three-Divisions research successor candidate;
- a separate 神異賦 transmission still preserves the non-contiguous geometry as 三停 in another lineage.

Therefore the old FR34 seven-anchor union is historically useful but is no longer the correct dependency contract for the contiguous 麻衣 successor.

## 2. Retained traditional anchors

FR262 requires exactly four traditional anchor contexts:

1. hairline
2. brow
3. zhuntou
4. dige

The following historical FR34 anchors are excluded from this successor:

- yintang
- shangen
- renzhong

This exclusion is methodological, not geometric.

It means only that those anchors are not required by the FR261 contiguous 麻衣 formula.

## 3. Contiguous span requirements

FR262 issues exactly three source-qualified span requirements:

- upper: hairline → brow
- middle: brow → zhuntou
- lower: zhuntou → dige

These are copied from the FR261 source-context adjudication and not reconstructed from FR33 winner-selection.

## 4. Historical FR34 preservation

FR34 remains unchanged.

Its seven-anchor union continues to record the earlier research coverage:

- hairline
- brow
- yintang
- shangen
- zhuntou
- renzhong
- dige

FR262 explicitly states:

`historicalFR34MayBeUsedAsSuccessor = false`

The historical artifact remains useful for provenance and for separate lineage research, including the non-contiguous formula.

Where possible, FR262 reuses FR34's provider-independent neutral requirement identifiers and unresolved dependency descriptions for the four retained anchors.

This reuse does not reuse FR34's source-variant interpretation.

It only avoids inventing duplicate neutral requirement names for the same physical research need.

## 6. Coordinate-frame decision

FR262 intentionally does **not** select a successor coordinate frame.

`canonical_image_normalized_2d`

`canonical_aligned_right_handed_metric_xy`

`unresolved_successor_frame_no_cross_frame_collapse`

A later derivation phase must decide whether to:

No conversion is invented here.

Before FR261/FR262, the 麻衣 Three-Divisions research path was forced to solve seven anchor contexts because FR33 treated two different source roles as rival variants of one method.

This removes yintang, shangen, renzhong and the philtrum surface from the contiguous successor without deleting them from the wider Face Reading research corpus.
