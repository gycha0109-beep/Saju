# FR185 — Eye-Pair Xi/Chang metric-to-concept mapping evidence requirements

## Scope

FR185 defines only the evidence admission contract that must be satisfied before any future review may authorize a semantic mapping from traditional `細` / `長` concepts to neutral Eye-Pair geometry metrics.

It does not admit a mapping, directionality, stable criterion identity, threshold, calibration, classifier, semantic output, or Production rule.

## Mapping evidence admission requirements

A future mapping review is not eligible to admit a binding unless all ten requirement classes below are represented by governed evidence or upstream authority.

### 1. Source concept identity and pinned provenance

The traditional concept must be anchored to the fixed direct-source witness and exact visually pinned source-page provenance. Translation or secondary-source wording cannot substitute for that identity.

For the current FR176 Daruma Eye witness, exact scan page `146` is already pinned. This satisfies provenance identity only; it does not satisfy semantic mapping.

### 2. Candidate neutral metric identity and versioned definition

The candidate metric must be an exact versioned neutral metric ref. Its coordinate frame, unit, denominator, aggregation rule, and semantic prohibitions must be frozen before correspondence review.

The current FR184 candidate metric identities satisfy this identity requirement only. Their geometric relevance does not make them traditional criteria.

### 3. Explicit mapping-hypothesis provenance

Before participant or expert evidence is collected, a governed hypothesis artifact must explicitly name:

- traditional concept;
- candidate neutral metric ref;
- exact traditional source refs;
- methodology ref;
- rationale for the proposed correspondence;
- a falsifiable correspondence claim.

A hypothesis cannot be reconstructed after observing metric/label results. Shared wording such as “thin” or “long,” geometric intuition, or FR184 candidate relevance is not sufficient provenance.

### 4. Independent blinded expert operationalization

The future evidence must reuse the repository `blinded_expert_operationalization` discipline.

Reviewers must operate from source-grounded instructions while blinded from:

- metric values;
- candidate thresholds;
- peer labels;
- fortune output.

Independent initial labels, abstention, and disagreement remain observable. The expert labeling process cannot be rewritten to fit the metric after results are known.

### 5. Repeat-capture stability

The exact candidate metric must have `repeat_capture_stability` evidence produced under a governed capture/quality protocol with independent recapture.

A metric that is not stable enough under the governed capture process cannot acquire traditional semantic authority merely because a single capture correlates with reviewer labels.

### 6. Source-grounded construct correspondence

The future mapping review must show correspondence between source-grounded expert operationalization and the exact candidate metric as the hypothesized construct.

This is not satisfied by:

- geometric resemblance asserted by developers;
- translation similarity;
- a single anecdotal example;
- post-hoc selection of whichever metric correlates best.

No correspondence statistic, direction, threshold, or acceptance cutoff is invented by FR185.

### 7. Alternative-metric and confound rejection

The review must test plausible alternative neutral metrics and documented capture/geometry confounds. The purpose is to prevent an arbitrary correlated metric or nuisance factor from becoming the traditional binding.

FR185 does not choose the alternative set, statistical method, or rejection threshold. Those require a future governed protocol.

### 8. End-to-end evidence traceability

Every input to a mapping decision must be traceable by exact refs, including as applicable:

- traditional source refs;
- methodology or hypothesis ref;
- neutral metric ref;
- capture / labeling / review protocol refs;
- dataset version;
- provenance refs;
- reviewed evidence refs.

Free-text paraphrase cannot replace the authority chain.

### 9. Explicit mapping acceptance or rejection decision

Evidence presence cannot self-promote a mapping.

A dedicated reviewed mapping decision must explicitly accept or reject the exact concept-to-metric relation and cite the complete admitted evidence set. Mapping admission and rejection must be reproducible from pinned refs.

### 10. Fail-closed completeness

If any required evidence, provenance link, independent review, alternative/confound check, or explicit decision authority is absent or below its required review state, the mapping remains `not_admitted`.

Requirements definition itself never counts as evidence satisfaction.

## Calibration evidence separation

FR185 preserves FR180's existing evidence classes but does not collapse mapping admission into threshold calibration.

Before mapping admission review, the contract requires evidence compatible with:

- `repeat_capture_stability`;
- `blinded_expert_operationalization`.

`threshold_selection_result` remains later calibration evidence. FR185 does not create it, require it merely to define the mapping-requirements contract, or allow it to retroactively supply source semantics.

Even a future admitted mapping would still leave separate authority work for:

- metric directionality;
- stable criterion identity;
- criterion-specific calibration evidence;
- criterion-specific calibration protocol;
- calibrated decision rule.
