# FE041H — Governed Neutral Candidate Canonicalization Policy

Watchtower-Track: `face-reading`

## Why this phase exists

FE041D/FE041F established that the three FR142 square/broad `方` candidates have an exact metricRef intersection of **0** with the FE035B 13-metric product-neutral registry.

That fact is necessary but not sufficient for product canonicalization.

A new neutral metric must not enter the canonical product surface merely because:

- it is implemented;
- it is deterministic on synthetic fixtures;
- it has a unique metricRef;
- it was designed from a source-grounded research question;
- it might later correlate with a traditional concept.

FE041H therefore defines the **neutral product-registry admission policy** before any successor registry is created.

## FE035B evolution boundary

The current product-neutral observation contract remains authoritative:

- contract: `FE035B-PRODUCT-NEUTRAL-OBSERVATION-CONTRACT-v1`
- canonical metrics: `13`
- v1 in-place mutation: forbidden
- new neutral metric: requires a new contract version
- registry membership: never implies traditional semantic authority

FE041H does not mutate FE035B.

## Required canonicalization evidence

A candidate must satisfy all eight requirement classes before registry admission.

### Already satisfied — 3/8

1. **Exact candidate identity + versioned neutral definition**
   - FR142 supplies immutable metric refs, versioned definitions, source surface, formula, unit, and runtime identity.

2. **Closed neutral semantic boundary**
   - FR142 explicitly leaves traditional binding, threshold, calibration, and classification null/unauthorized.

3. **Deterministic runtime + synthetic verification**
   - FR143 verifies deterministic synthetic numeric behavior while explicitly denying that synthetic evidence is empirical repeatability or construct validity.

### Still unresolved — 5/8

4. **Explicit product region/unit/presence/unavailable-surface contract**
   - The candidates have neutral source geometry and a unit, but they do not yet have an issued FE035B-style product contract assigning `regionKey`, `required|conditional` presence, and unavailable-surface behavior.
   - FE041H does not infer `mouth_lips` merely from the source surface name.

5. **Empirical repeat-capture + capture-quality evidence**
   - Synthetic determinism is not product capture stability.
   - Governed empirical evidence is still required.

6. **Duplicate/redundancy review against the current registry**
   - Exact metricRef intersection is zero.
   - Zero identity overlap does **not** prove the new metric adds a non-redundant product observation surface.

7. **Successor contract version + migration design**
   - FE035B v1 cannot be edited in place.
   - No successor contract version or compatibility/migration design is issued here.

8. **Explicit registry-admission decision + end-to-end provenance**
   - No governed decision currently binds all required evidence into an admission result.

Current canonicalization readiness is therefore:

```
3 / 8 satisfied
5 / 8 unresolved
candidate canonicalization = NOT AUTHORIZED
successor registry = NOT ISSUED
```

## Prohibited shortcuts

FE041H explicitly rejects:

- structural append validity → governed admission;
- unique metricRef → product admission;
- zero exact overlap → non-redundancy;
- synthetic repeatability → empirical product stability;
- canonical neutral membership → traditional binding;
- canonicalization policy definition → candidate canonicalization;
- successor planning → successor registry issuance.

## Hard boundary

- FE035B mutation;

- directionality;
- threshold, percentile, calibration;
