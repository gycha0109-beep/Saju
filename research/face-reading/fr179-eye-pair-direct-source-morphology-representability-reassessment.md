## What changed

The repeated FR175/FR176 need for a role-invariant vertical-to-horizontal geometric shape ratio is no longer missing. Therefore the earlier observation-level gap for `或細長極寸` and `細而長` is narrowed.

That does **not** make either source clause directly representable.

The new Y:X ratio does not define when a measured ratio becomes traditional `細`. The pre-existing relative X-span does not define when a measured span becomes traditional `長`. No source-authorized compound rule defines `細而長`. FR178 also supplies no meaning for `極` and no conversion from normalized/canonical geometry to traditional `寸`.

This follows the same authority discipline as FR81/FR83: a neutral shape or size observation can be relevant to a source concept without constituting a traditional metric binding.

Still blocked by:

### `細而長`

No source-authorized thresholds or semantic mapping exist for `細`, `長`, or their conjunction. Binding remains not admitted.

### `目長一寸`

### `目有三角`

### `目大而光`

A partial relative-size observation cannot discard `光`. Ocular radiance remains outside the static geometry authority, and selective decomposition is forbidden.

### `目尾相垂`

No reviewed eye-tail endpoint orientation primitive exists. FR178 does not assign endpoint roles or anatomical laterality.

### `秀而正`

FR178 does not operationalize `秀` or source-authorized `正` form semantics.
