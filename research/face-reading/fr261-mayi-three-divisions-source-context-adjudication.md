# FR261 — 麻衣 三停 Source-Context Adjudication

Watchtower-Track: face-research

## 1. Purpose

FR261 revisits the source context behind FR33.

FR33 preserved two scan-checked geometric triplets from the 1925 NLC 麻衣相法 witness as two unresolved 三停 boundary variants:

1. 髮際→印堂 / 山根→準頭 / 人中→地閣
2. 髮際→眉 / 眉→準頭 / 準頭→地閣

That preservation was useful, but the subsequent winner-selection framing is too coarse.

Direct scan re-review and independent transmission evidence show that the two triplets do not occupy the same textual role inside the relevant 麻衣 passage.

FR261 therefore preserves FR33 as a historical research artifact and issues a successor source-context adjudication.

## 2. Direct 1925 麻衣相法 witness re-review

Witness:

- work: 麻衣相法
- volume: 卷一
- section context: 三才三停論
- witness: 國家圖書館 / 1925 NLC scan already used by FR33
- FR33 scan window: pages 35–36

The passage sequence distinguishes two roles.

### First triplet — 三府 / 三主 context

The source sequence reads the non-contiguous spans as:

- 髮際至印堂 → 上府 → 初主
- 山根至準頭 → 中府 → 中主
- 人中至地閣 → 下府 → 末主

The important point is not merely the geometry.

The same clauses explicitly attach 府 and 主 roles.

FR261 therefore classifies this triplet as:

`three_governors_three_fus_context_not_mayi_three_divisions_boundary`

It is no longer eligible to compete against the contiguous triplet as a second 麻衣 三停 geometry.

### Second triplet — explicit 三停 context

Immediately following the first triplet, the witness gives:

- 自髮際至眉為上停
- 眉至準頭為中停
- 準頭至地閣為下停

This is the triplet explicitly labeled 上停 / 中停 / 下停 in the same source context.

FR261 therefore retains this as the 麻衣 三停 research-methodology successor candidate.

This does not authorize a Production region map.

## 3. Why FR33 is not rewritten

FR33 remains useful as a record of the earlier ambiguity and the six scan-checked spans that triggered the research.

FR261 does not mutate FR33.

Instead:

- FR33 remains historical.
- FR261 supersedes only the interpretation that the two triplets are rival boundary variants of one 麻衣 三停 method.
- downstream successor contracts must cite FR261 when they narrow the 麻衣 dependency set.

## 4. Independent transmission corroboration

### 欽定古今圖書集成

The 藝術典 transmission preserves the same local ordering:

- non-contiguous spans as 上府 / 中府 / 下府 with 初主 / 中主 / 末主;
- followed by the contiguous 上停 / 中停 / 下停 formula.

Research locator:

https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/欽定古今圖書集成/博物彙編/藝術典/第632卷

This source is corroborative. It is not used as the sole authority for correcting the project’s 1925 麻衣 witness.

### 神相全編 transmission

A separate 神相全編 transmission also preserves a contiguous face 三停 formula:

- 髮際→眉
- 眉→準頭
- 準頭→地閣

Research locator:

https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/欽定古今圖書集成/博物彙編/藝術典/第633卷

This supports the existence of the contiguous Three-Divisions tradition but is not merged into the 麻衣 lineage.

### 神異賦 transmission

A separate 神異賦 transmission uses the non-contiguous triplet as face 三停 under the statement 三停平等:

- 髮際→印堂
- 山根→準頭
- 人中→地閣

Research locator:

https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/欽定古今圖書集成/博物彙編/藝術典/第636卷

This is why FR261 does **not** delete or globally reject the non-contiguous Three-Divisions formula.

Instead it preserves it as:

`separate_lineage_candidate_pending_repository_source_pinning`

The current external transcription is not automatically admitted into the project authority registry and is not allowed to overwrite the 麻衣 source context.

## 5. Methodology decision

For the 1925 麻衣 witness only, the Three-Divisions research formula becomes:

`hairline → brow → zhuntou → dige`

or equivalently:

- upper: hairline → brow
- middle: brow → zhuntou
- lower: zhuntou → dige

The old question:

> Which of the two FR33 variants should win?

is retired for the 麻衣 methodology.

The new structure is:

- 麻衣 三府/三主: non-contiguous triplet, separate role.
- 麻衣 三停: contiguous triplet.
- 神異賦 三停: non-contiguous candidate in a separate lineage.

No universal formula is issued.

## 6. Downstream impact

This changes the research dependency graph materially.

FR34 currently carries a seven-anchor union because it was built to support both FR33 variants:

- hairline
- brow
- yintang
- shangen
- zhuntou
- renzhong
- dige

For the FR261 麻衣 contiguous successor, only four traditional anchors remain structurally required:

- hairline
- brow
- zhuntou
- dige

Therefore:

- yintang is not required by the 麻衣 contiguous successor;
- shangen is not required by the 麻衣 contiguous successor;
- renzhong is not required by the 麻衣 contiguous successor;
- the FR35 philtrum surface is not a required dependency for that successor;
- the old FR34 seven-anchor union must not be reused as the successor contract;
- the old FR36 seven-derivation union must not be reused as the successor derivation contract.

This removes research work that was created by conflating two textual contexts.
