## Authority and scope

The selected [子平真詮/評註 chapter](https://www.ncc.com.tw/fate/paleo/bg/bg_032.htm)
was reread on 2026-10-07. Its support-family context and distinction between
visible repetition and roots motivate preserving separate layers. The text does
not supply an inventory serialization, complete effective-support algorithm or
numeric threshold. R31's joint inspection is an explicit project research policy;
it is not inferred automatically from a quotation or upstream collection outputs.

R31 binds unchanged R23/R28/R30/R6 evidence definitions and their governing
authority hashes. Existing category source access dates are inherited accurately;
no fresh category-source validation is claimed. Upstream restrictions remain
unchanged. The new authority authorizes joint inspection only:

- visible year/month/hour observations, including labels outside admitted families;
- all R30 hidden occurrences in year/month/day/hour branches, including outside-scope labels;
- R6 positive bounded root facets in a **separate** per-branch context.

Visible occurrence IDs are `visible:<slot>`; hidden IDs are
`hidden:<slot>:<stem>` and retain the original upstream ID. Repeated stems across
slots and across visible/hidden layers are distinct. Visible day self and branch
representative Ten-Gods are excluded. No Ten-God recomputation or root rediscovery.

A root facet is not another occurrence, even when several governed root kinds
describe one branch. Linking root facets and hidden IDs by branch means only
co-location. R31 does not identify an individual hidden stem as a root cause,
deduplicate semantic effects, add the layers or assign weight.

`symbolicOccurrenceDomainResolved=true` closes only the admitted input-domain
coverage. It does not mean complete whole-chart assistance, complete 通根, hidden
manifestation/activation or usable support. In particular yin 長生 conflict,
yin/Earth 祿, Earth 餘氣 and negative absence remain unresolved. Empty bounded
root facets do not mean 無根. Resolved no-positive inventory does not mean no support;
missing evidence or a missing claim is not a negative verdict.

## Execution and fail-closed boundaries

`collectGovernedBiyinSupportInventory(snapshot)` builds the four upstream
envelopes and independently validates each against the same snapshot. Every
visible slot must agree between R23 and R28 on canonical label/source and cannot
belong to both families. Missing ID/hash, unmaterialized scenarios, ambiguity,
unavailable inputs, master/day-pillar parity, hidden membership/source mismatch,
upstream forgery or unresolved root pillar aborts the entire inventory.

The projection preserves all upstream envelope IDs, definitions, versions and
payload hashes; each occurrence and branch context retains provenance.
The R31 envelope binds snapshot ID/hash and replays the whole projection.
Rehashed omissions, duplicate/renamed identities, source/branch crosslinks,
root insertion into occurrences, root-facet removal, extra counts or promoted
authority fail runtime admission. Generic envelope hashing alone is insufficient.

An isolated registry emits a non-narrative T2 positive marker when an admitted
symbolic constituent exists. Valid no-positive evidence and absent evidence both
produce no positive marker, while their execution/evidence states remain distinct.
No default registry or Production pack is activated; Production promotion fails.

R25 count is not consumed. R29 and its frontier are unchanged. There is no count,
score, weight, addition, usable support/effect composition, 黨眾/助寡, 旺衰/強弱,
格局, 調候 or 喜用忌 inference. No canonical fact, public API/export, schema,
Product DB, provider, auth, deployment or product route changes.
`ReadingIntent → Profile → Claims → Evidence → Narrative` remains the only product
path. Shared effect/classifier authority and actual full-chain product consumption
and smoke remain Production blockers; A/B/C completion is not asserted.
