## Data flow and consumer

Canonical Snapshot → pinned master metadata/day-stem parity and month-branch
metadata validation → existing matcher → research envelope bound to exact snapshot
ID/hash → full independent projection replay → isolated registered T2 timing claim.

Implementation:

- `shared-natal-wood-month-command-timing-research-evidence-adapter.ts`: projector,
  envelope producer and replay validator/runtime adapter.
- `shared-natal-wood-month-command-timing-structural-claim.ts`: two closed claim
  values, one registered claim type and isolated research registry/pack.

Only `derivedFacts.dayMaster`, `pillars.day.stem` (parity) and
`pillars.month.branch` enter the predicate. Metadata is checked against pinned
manseryeok 2.0.0 stem/branch value, element, Yin/Yang and Hanja. Year/hour pillars,
day branch, hidden/root/TenGod evidence and Twelve Growth Stage are not inputs.
The replay checks all payload fields and the unchanged authority hash, so merely
rehashing a forged verdict, source, identity, extra field or promoted constraint
cannot admit it. Exact envelope ID and snapshot ID/hash remain independently bound.

Actual consumer is `runInterpretation` with this isolated research registry and
runtime adapter. The engine records the envelope reference on the T2 claim. It is
not inserted into the default registry or actual product reading route.

Direct product audit: `product-reading.ts` → `product-reading-service.ts` →
`governed-reading-execution.ts`. General natal intent includes a strength target,
but that is not classifier authority. The detailed general presentation consumes
I18A month-branch Five-Element relationship and explicitly prohibits a strength
classifier. I18A relationship is distinct from month-command timing and is not
replaced. Existing ReadingIntent → Profile → Claims → Evidence → Narrative full
consumption and product interpretation smoke remain incomplete.
