## Source and methodology adoption

The selected [子平真詮/評註 chapter](https://www.ncc.com.tw/fate/paleo/bg/bg_032.htm)
was reread on 2026-10-07. It associates 比劫/印綬/通根 with support, explicitly
discusses branch 比印 (`年日時支，又通根比印`) and allows all four pillars to
modify the picture (`年月日時，亦有損益之權`).

**Declared project interpretation:** those clauses justify admitting already
mapped hidden 比肩/劫財/正印/偏印 occurrences as _symbolic constituents_ within
the separately audited four-branch universe. The four-slot inventory domain
comes from R26/R27; the text's illustrative year/day/hour clause is not claimed
to be a standalone exhaustive four-slot algorithm. R30 adopts the bounded scope
explicitly instead of pretending the quotation or R27 mapping supplies authority
automatically. This is a contested research methodology, not universal doctrine.

Existing category authorities supply label meanings only: 比肩/劫財 under 比劫
and 正印/偏印 under 印綬. Their sources/hashes are bound to this decision. The
千里命稿 category naming is not imported as a root, precedence or effect doctrine.
No canonical FactState is fabricated for a derived R27 relationship; R30 uses
the mapped occurrence label directly under its own scope policy.

The admission means only **a named support-family constituent is structurally
present**. It cannot mean hidden manifestation, usable assistance, activated
support, 通根, weight, final composition or 黨眾. Every other mapped label stays
in the inventory as `outside_admitted_support_label_scope`; that is not a
universal negative support verdict.

## Execution contract

`collectHiddenBiyinSupportConstituents(snapshot)`:

1. Builds unchanged R27 evidence and independently replays it against the same
   snapshot. Missing/unavailable/ambiguous source, scenarios, master parity,
   membership/order/duplicate mismatch or upstream forgery yields unavailable.
2. Consumes the governed occurrences without a new `getTenGod` call, canonical
   table, branch representative scan or visible-slot selector.
3. Preserves every occurrence's `(pillar slot, hidden stem)` ID, branch, mapped
   Ten-God and source fact/pillar refs; marks only the four admitted labels.
4. Includes day branch and preserves repeated stems at different slots; visible
   day self and representative branch are not additional members.
5. Records the upstream envelope identity/definition/version/payload hash and
   explicit authority constraints in an exact snapshot-ID/hash-bound envelope.
6. Replays the entire payload during validation. Rehashed label/category,
   source/identity, member omission, upstream ref, count or authority tampering
   cannot pass the runtime gate.

The isolated research registry emits a registered T2 marker only when at least
one admitted constituent exists. Detailed occurrences remain in its validated
evidence. A resolved outside-scope inventory and missing evidence both emit no
positive marker, but they remain distinct states; missing claim is not negative.
The claim is non-material for Narrative and Production pack promotion is rejected.
