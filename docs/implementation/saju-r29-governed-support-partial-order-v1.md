## Execution and evidence

`composeGovernedSupportPartialOrder(snapshot)`:

1. Requires snapshot ID/hash, materialized scenario, resolved day master and all
   four resolved pillars, with exact day-master/day-pillar stem-fact parity.
2. Builds and independently validates R6, R23 and R28 evidence against that same
   snapshot; any unavailable or non-replayable input aborts the entire operation.
3. Requires R23/R28 canonical/source-slot parity and disjoint positive families.
4. Preserves each support observation's ID, pillar, family, member/root kind,
   source fact and upstream envelope ID. Same branch/member at different slots
   remains separate provenance; duplicates of an observation identity are rejected.
5. Supplies only eligible classes to unchanged I22. Root comparisons retain the
   full dominated-evidence trace; incomparable maximal pairs remain explicit.
6. Keeps 劫財 observations and IDs outside the **eligible-observation** frontier
   with unresolved precedence. An empty eligible frontier is not no support.

The projection includes all observations, eligible frontier, unranked IDs,
upstream envelope/definition/payload-hash references, coverage and constraints.
The validator replays the entire projection; rehashing deleted unranked evidence,
forged rank/frontier/provenance, extra scalar or promoted authority cannot bypass it.

Missing or ambiguous input cannot become an empty observation set. A valid
empty bounded composition emits no T2 marker and cannot prove 無根/助寡/weak.
Positive evidence emits one registered research-only T2 structural marker with
its detailed composition preserved in the evidence envelope. The marker is
non-material for Narrative; absent claim is not a negative conclusion.
