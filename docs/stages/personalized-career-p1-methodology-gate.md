## Hard boundaries

- Missing claim is not negative evidence.
- Occurrence count is not magnitude, rank, or score.
- Visible-stem positional wording is not a numeric weight.
- Visible-stem semantics do not automatically apply to hidden stems.
- Source vocabulary does not become a Career outcome without a Registry-bound rule and reviewed methodology.
- Legacy Career T8 and personalized Career T8 may not be mixed in one pack.
- No occupation assignment, salary prediction, promotion prediction, future timing, or numeric Career score.

## What would change this gate

A later gate may promote a `preserve_only` or `blocked` dimension only after an explicit source/methodology review identifies the exact semantic rule and scope. Architecture requirements, developer intuition, model knowledge, or the existence of a structural fact are not sufficient authority.
