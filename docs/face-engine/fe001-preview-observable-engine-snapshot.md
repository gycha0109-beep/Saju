## Availability behavior

Child modules keep their own fail-closed behavior.

FE001 does not invent substitute values. Optional unavailable child outputs are summarized under `availability.unavailableRefs`.

- no unavailable child surface: `complete`
- one or more unavailable child surfaces: `partial`

## Authority boundary

FE001 is preview observable composition only.

It does not:

- decide research validity;
- decide validation status;
- infer anatomy;
- invent thresholds or classifiers;
- bind traditional physiognomy rules;
- generate physiognomy or fortune claims;
- activate Production or Commerce.

The frozen package `index.ts` is not modified.
