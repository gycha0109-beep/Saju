## Verified boundary

The selected corpus supports Day as a reading surface for:
- SELF
- SPOUSE / wife-concubine domain in the source's historical terminology

It also supplies configuration-specific spouse claims. R063 preserves two bounded examples separately from the broad Day-domain claims:
- `日坐命財更在財生旺之鄉主得妻財又主妻賢明`
- `或日為妻如在空刑克煞之地言克妻妾之斷`

These examples remain non-executable and do not establish a universal spouse outcome rule.

However, these passages do not by themselves establish a universal canonical primitive:

`DAY_BRANCH = SPOUSE_PALACE`

That narrower ontology requires direct source governance of the branch-specific claim rather than inference from the broader Day-domain language.
