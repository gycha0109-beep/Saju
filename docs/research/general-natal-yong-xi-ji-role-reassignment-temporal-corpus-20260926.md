## Frontier

R136 implements the R100 frontier:

> Yong/Xi/Ji role-reassignment temporal corpus

The purpose is not to build a temporal Yongshen selector. It is to preserve the semantic difference between a natal role, a luck-period interaction with that role, temporary activation, structural change, and an actually evidenced role reassignment.

## Governed upstream

R136 composes existing governed repository evidence rather than introducing a new universal traditional proposition.

- **R032/R033**: 喜神 != 用神; 忌神 is contextual. The direct control `財化為官，忌神變為喜神` proves that a governed relation can change role, but it does not itself prove a generic temporal switcher.
- **R040**: methodology-specific Yong/Xi/Ji evidence must not collapse into one final Yongshen.
- **R072**: Dayun interacts with natal structure; completion/change and natal rescue/blocking are configuration-specific.
- **R073**: latent natal material can become operative under luck, sometimes temporarily; activation is not permanent natal mutation or event authority.
- **R076**: break/rescue depends on exact configuration and counterforce.
- **R077**: luck can support or oppose natal Yong/Xi and can participate in structural completion/change; automatic temporal Yongshen reselection is explicitly rejected.
- **R135**: structural transition observations do not authorize temporal transition semantics or a transition resolver.

Primary repository source surface for R032/R033/R072/R073/R076/R077: 子平真詮評註 commentary material already verified by those frontiers.

## Corpus shape

R136 records **24 probes across 11 comparison groups**.

### Retained natal role interaction

Six probes preserve ordinary support/opposition as interaction with an existing natal role. They do not emit a new role identity.

Examples:

```text
natal YONG + luck support -> YONG retained/supported
natal XI   + luck support -> XI retained/supported
natal YONG + luck opposition -> YONG retained/opposed
natal XI   + luck opposition -> XI retained/opposed
```

The same nominal support item is also replayed under different natal context to preserve R072/R077's rule that natal material can block or redirect the apparent effect.

### Latent activation without reassignment

Four probes cover latent XI/JI material activated by transparency or natal+luck meeting.

```text
NATAL_LATENT_XI -> TEMPORARILY_OPERATIVE_SAME_ROLE(XI)
NATAL_LATENT_JI -> TEMPORARILY_OPERATIVE_SAME_ROLE(JI)
```

Activation changes operative state, not semantic role identity. The corpus therefore records the temporary/permanent boundary explicitly.

### Structural completion/change

YONG, XI, and JI baselines are each tested against:

- luck completing an incomplete natal structure;
- luck meeting/clash changing the structure.

All six rows end at `ROLE_REASSIGNMENT_UNRESOLVED`. R072/R077 establish that structural completion/change can occur, but they do not authorize the inference:

```text
structural change -> automatic Yong/Xi/Ji reassignment
```

### Rescue/blocking counterexamples

R076 cases are preserved as counterexamples to naive role switching:

- hidden 傷官 exposure can damage an officer-use configuration;
- natal 印 can protect 官 under the same apparent trigger;
- a luck-completed meeting can be blocked by natal metal counterforce;
- `逢成格而不喜`;
- `逢變格而不忌`.

These rows preserve context dependence instead of creating a global break/recovery toggle.

### R033 source-governed role-change control

The corpus includes the direct non-temporal control:

```text
財化為官，忌神變為喜神
JI -> XI
```

This is the only row where a role reassignment is recorded as observed. A paired synthetic temporal analogue is deliberately `INDETERMINATE` because the source-governed control does not authorize extrapolation from “some temporal structural change” to the same Ji->Xi transition.

## Rejected collapses

R136 explicitly rejects:

- every Dayun/year/month reselects Yongshen;
- a fixed element is permanently Xi or Ji;
- support/opposition creates a new Yongshen identity;
- latent activation equals role reassignment;
- structural change automatically reassigns Yong/Xi/Ji;
- the R033 Ji->Xi control is a generic temporal switcher;
- temporary operative state is permanent natal mutation;
- the same luck item has the same role outcome across charts;
- role reassignment implies a concrete event;
- scalar role scores, probabilities, or confidence ranking.
