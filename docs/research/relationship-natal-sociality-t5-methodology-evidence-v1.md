## 2. Relevant source passages

- **Page 31, section 5.1:** Introduces the modern Ten-God concept and discusses sociality through positive and cautionary expressions associated with individual Ten Gods.
- **Page 32, Tables 1–2:** Describes sociality functions by the five Ten-God families and presents a staged account of social tendencies. The tables provide a vocabulary for individual/family-level sociality facets, not a relationship-composition algorithm.
- **Page 33, Table 3 and surrounding discussion:** Connects the distribution and relative effects of Ten Gods with human relations, social activity, and competitiveness. It still does not define deterministic relationship outcomes or an executable multi-factor rule.

The paper explicitly contrasts older family-role-only interpretations with a modern expansion of Ten-God meaning. Its stated purpose is broader than kinship assignment. That supports a bounded **general sociality** research facet.

## 3. What the source supports

The article supports a source-pinned description of individual Ten-God sociality facets, including both constructive expressions and cautionary expressions. The implementation therefore records one exact observed Ten-God as one T5 facet.

The candidate preserves the engine's visible-stem and branch channels separately. This is an implementation-level provenance distinction; the paper does not authorize separate channel weights or a numeric comparison between channels.

## 4. What the source does not support

This paper does **not** define:

- A multi-Ten-God relationship synthesis rule or a T8 general-relationship conclusion
- Relationship quality, compatibility, marriage, romance, partner identity, or spouse characteristics
- Family-role or gender-role assignment for a real person
- A numeric score, dominance measure, frequency threshold, or probability
- A clinical, psychometric, or diagnostic interpretation of the psychological terminology used in the article
- Future events or deterministic outcomes

The paper is a secondary synthesis of Kim Ki-seung's modern Myeongri work. The cited underlying books were not independently inspected. No new T8 rule is authorized by this source.
