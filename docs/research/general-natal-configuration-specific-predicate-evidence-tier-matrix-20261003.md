## Scope

R165 compares the three R076 configuration-specific temporal mechanisms:

- break trigger;
- natal rescue;
- counterforce.

The comparison is not a ranking. It separates source-case replay material from
surfaces that are explicit enough to justify further predicate research.

No row is promoted into an executable semantic rule.

## Upstream boundary

R165 binds:

- R076 configuration-specific paraphrased cases;
- R157 trigger-class provenance;
- R159 sufficiency fail-closed audit;
- R163 paired break/rescue textual delta;
- R164 counterforce grouped-channel replay.

R159 remains controlling for the key negative result: none of these mechanisms
has an exact minimal predicate set, matching sufficiency, bounded outcome
sufficiency, or settlement sufficiency.

## Follow-up research candidates

Exactly three surfaces are marked as follow-up predicate-research candidates:

- 命有甲;
- 庚辛;
- 申酉.

This flag is an acquisition/research-routing signal only. It does not establish
semantic authority.

A stronger predicate would still require new source-backed evidence sufficient
to answer at least:

- exact matching scope;
- required versus optional context;
- minimality;
- sufficiency;
- competing-force precedence;
- final settlement.

## Prohibited interpretations

R165 does not authorize:

- evidence classes as authority ranks;
- mechanism strength ranking;
- candidate counts as numeric weights;
- omission as proven absence;
- Jia presence as automatic rescue;
- grouped metal surfaces as sufficient counterforce;
- Mao/Wei as a complete meeting matcher;
- source outcome wording as universal settlement;
- executable trigger resolution;
- InterpretationClaim emission;
- preview or production promotion.

## Next frontier

The useful next step is not another inferred rule.

For the three positive candidate surfaces, the next research should seek direct
or better-bounded source witnesses and variant/corroborating context that can
test whether any candidate can satisfy a stricter predicate contract.

Until then, all three remain research-routing candidates only.
