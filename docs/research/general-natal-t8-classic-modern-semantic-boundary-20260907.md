## 2. What the public scholarly abstract establishes

The source's stated purpose is to examine the origin and meaning of Ten-God / Six-Relative concepts in major classics and trace how the concept expanded in modern Myeongri practice.

The public abstract explicitly distinguishes two layers:

```text
major classics including
淵海子平 / 三命通會 / 子平真詮
→ Ten-God content described by the authors as mostly limited to family/kin relationship concepts

later modern development
→ moves beyond family assignment toward Ten-God operation
→ later authors extend the concept toward psychology and aptitude
```

The abstract specifically identifies the later modern expansion toward human psychology / innate aptitude as a development beyond the earlier classical family-relation framing.

Therefore the public scholarly surface supports the following methodological boundary:

```text
classical Ten-God relation/nomenclature
!= automatically the same semantic authority as
modern psychology / aptitude interpretation
```
