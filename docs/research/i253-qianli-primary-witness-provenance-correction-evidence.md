## 1. Why this correction exists

The historical public-classic frontier treated two surfaces as one Qianli work family:

1. the 1935 National Library of China scan titled `千里命稿`, and
2. a public CText theoretical transcription containing `明暗`, `地位`, `支冲`, and the later `本氣 / 時令 / 多寡` discussion.

Direct inspection of primary NLC witnesses shows that this same-work assertion is not reliable.

The NLC catalog exposes two distinct works by 韋千里 from the same publisher:

- `千里命稿`, 韋氏命苑, 1935, NLC `nlc:data_416,01jh000372,10197`, 123 scan pages;
- `韋千里命學講義`, 韋氏命苑, 1936, NLC `nlc:data_416,01jh000368,10155`, 368 scan pages / 346 physical pages.

The 1936 `韋千里命學講義` catalog explicitly lists 卷五 `補充篇` and `評斷篇`. The theoretical stream previously attributed to the 1935 `千里命稿` aligns with this 卷五 structure instead.

Therefore the old `1935 千里命稿 scan + CText theoretical stream = same work` assertion is superseded for all future authority evaluation. Historical I251/I252 remain intact as audit records; they are not rewritten as if the error never occurred.
