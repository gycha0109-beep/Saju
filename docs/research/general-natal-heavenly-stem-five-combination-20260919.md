## Three-stage boundary

The source distinguishes:

1. **PAIR IDENTITY** — the two stems are one of the canonical Five-Combination pairs.
2. **EFFECTIVE COMBINATION** — the concrete arrangement actually permits the pair to combine.
3. **TRANSFORMATION** — the effective combination has sufficient supporting conditions to transform.

These stages must not be collapsed.

## Material prerequisites / blockers

Direct examples and commentary make the following factors material:

- intervening / positional arrangement can prevent effective combination;
- a pair can combine without transforming;
- branch support and rooting can preserve original function even under combination;
- transformation requires supporting Earthly-Branch conditions;
- season / command support is material in 化氣 examples;
- whole-chart 化氣格 is a further classification and is not identical to pair transformation.
