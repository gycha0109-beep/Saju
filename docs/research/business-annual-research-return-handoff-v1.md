# Business Annual Research Return Handoff v1

Issue: #1761  
Track: `saju-bridge`  
Upstream Bridge review: #1569 / clean replacement PR #1759

## Purpose

The Business Annual authority review returned the current `0.1.0-research` candidate to traditional Saju Research without rejecting it.

The candidate is executable and bounded, but its Business Annual T9 interpretation semantics are grounded only in an internal policy source. The Annual rules remain research-only, heuristic, experimental, and unreviewed. The reused Business Natal specialist rules remain research-only, secondary-only, contested, and unreviewed.

This handoff converts that `RETURN_TO_RESEARCH` decision into a deterministic, content-addressed Research contract. It does not perform source research, preserve current Business Annual semantic keys as truth, or admit authority.

## Current candidate surface

The handoff binds directly from `createBusinessAnnualReadingCandidateRegistry()`:

- Business Annual candidate version: `0.1.0-research`;
- the cumulative interpretation pack;
- the complete current methodology surface;
- ten Business Annual stem Ten-God activation rules;
- four Annual-to-natal branch-clash tension rules;
- fourteen Business Annual T9 rules;
- eleven reused Business Natal specialist rules;
- the complete cumulative registry rule surface directly from the current content-addressed snapshot.

The cumulative rule total is not reconstructed with manual arithmetic. The handoff binds the actual registry snapshot and checks:

```text
candidateBinding.ruleCount
==
candidateBinding.registryRuleCount
==
actual registry.snapshot.rules.length
```

The candidate-surface hash covers the exact candidate version, pack, sorted methodology references, and sorted rule references.

Research may narrow, change, split, or remove unsupported semantics. Any candidate drift requires a later Bridge review to bind a fresh content-addressed surface.

## Research workstream A — Business Annual stem Ten-God operating semantic authority

Research must establish what Business Annual-specific operating interpretation, if any, is source-qualified for the request-resolved Annual stem Ten-God.

Current Business axes and semantic keys are candidate semantics only.

For each retained proposition family, Research must preserve:

- Source Statement;
- Interpretive Reading;
- Research Inference;
- scope and preconditions;
- allowed meaning and meaning strength;
- qualifiers and exceptions;
- counterexamples;
- school dependencies;
- non-implications;
- source and provenance references.

An Annual stem Ten-God must not become a guarantee of business success, revenue, profit, funding, failure, bankruptcy, market events, industry outcome, founder suitability, or any specific business event.

## Research workstream B — Annual-to-natal branch-clash operating-adjustment authority

The resolved branch-relation fact and its Business interpretation are separate.

Research must determine whether Annual-to-natal branch clash supports a bounded Business Annual operating-adjustment meaning and, if so, under which conditions and with what strength.

Clash alone must not be promoted into business failure, bankruptcy, revenue loss, funding failure, partner breakup, market events, or another concrete business event.

## Research workstream C — Business axes, adjustment areas, and emphasis

The current candidate uses Business-specific axes, adjustment areas, primary activation emphasis, and pillar-specific clash emphasis.

These are not pre-approved authority.

Research must verify, narrow, replace, or remove them according to source-qualified evidence.

## Research workstream D — Business Annual scope and qualifier contract

Authority scopes remain distinct:

```text
Business Natal Authority != Business Annual Authority
General Annual Authority != Business Annual Authority
Career Annual Authority != Business Annual Authority
Wealth Annual Authority != Business Annual Authority
Business Annual Authority != Business Monthly Authority
```

Research must return explicit scope, required inputs, preconditions, allowed conclusions, qualifiers, exceptions, counterexamples, school dependencies, prohibited extensions, and unresolved questions.

## Research workstream E — Cross-scope claim-level reuse boundary

Any reused authority must be reviewed claim-by-claim.

No wholesale authority inheritance is allowed from:

- Business Natal;
- General Annual;
- Career Annual;
- Wealth Annual.

Business Annual Research success does not authorize Business Monthly semantics.

## Research workstream F — Product policy / Saju semantics / business outcomes / financial advice

Internal Business Annual policy, request packaging, executable fixtures, Reading Profile coverage, and temporal fact derivation may support product behavior and testability.

They are not standalone traditional Saju semantic authority.

Traditional Saju semantic authority is separate from both:

- deterministic business-outcome prediction;
- financial or investment advice authorization.

No output from this handoff authorizes investment, tax, credit, debt, insurance, securities, funding, or other financial advice.
