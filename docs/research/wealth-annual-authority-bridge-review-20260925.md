The candidate consumes:

```text

```

These are temporal prerequisites.

They do not establish:

- that an Annual stem Ten-God has the current wealth-management meaning;
- that the current Wealth axis or semantic key is authoritative;
- that Annual branch clash means financial-plan adjustment pressure;
- that the current adjustment areas are authoritative;
- that activation deserves `major` emphasis;

Executable precision, fixtures, and Reading Profile coverage are not semantic authority.

## Financial safety boundary

Current candidate outputs explicitly deny financial-advice authorization.

This review preserves that boundary. It does not authorize:

- income or salary prediction;
- investment-return prediction;
- loss prediction;
- debt-event prediction;
- windfall prediction;
- market-event prediction;
- specific financial-event prediction;
- investment, tax, credit, debt, insurance, or other financial advice.

A bounded Saju interpretation claim and regulated or practical financial advice are separate authority domains.

## Allowed conclusion

The current repository supports only these conclusions:

1. the Wealth Annual candidate is technically representable and executable as research;
2. annual temporal facts can provide bounded input evidence;
3. Wealth Natal research claims can coexist in the pack without conferring Wealth Annual authority;
4. the candidate already encodes strong non-prediction and non-advice boundaries;
5. Wealth Annual-specific interpretation semantics and current emphasis choices are not yet established as authority.

The Bridge therefore does not rewrite the candidate. It returns the semantic authority gap to Research.

## Research return

Research must establish, with source-qualified evidence:

1. the admissible meaning and strength of Wealth Annual stem Ten-God money-management semantics;
2. the admissible meaning and strength of annual-to-natal branch-clash financial-plan adjustment semantics, if retained;
3. whether the current Wealth axes, semantic keys, adjustment areas, activation emphasis, and pillar-specific clash emphasis have source support or must be narrowed/removed;
4. Wealth Annual-specific scope, qualifiers, exceptions, counterexamples, school dependencies, and non-implications;
5. whether any Wealth Natal, General Annual, or Career Annual authority is reusable claim-by-claim;
6. a clean separation between product policy, traditional Saju semantic authority, and financial advice.
