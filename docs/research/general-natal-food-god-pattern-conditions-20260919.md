## Source

子平真詮 / 子平真詮評註 — 論用神成敗救應

https://www.ncc.com.tw/fate/paleo/bg/bg_033.htm

## 成

```text
食神生財
食帶煞而無財
棄食就煞而透印
```

The commentary is especially important here: when 煞 is strong and 印 is exposed, `棄食就煞` may mean that although the month command is 食神, the chart is no longer treated as an ordinary 食神格.

## 敗

```text
食神逢梟
生財露煞
```

## 帶忌

```text
食神帶煞印而又逢財
```

## 救應

```text
食逢梟而就煞以成格
或生財以護食
```

## Boundary

This source directly disproves a simple `月令食神 => 食神格成立` implementation.

Candidate selection, ordinary 食神格, 食神制煞, 棄食就煞, contamination, and rescue are distinct states. The transition predicates are not yet all governed.

No final 格局 establishment, Production fact, SKU, or Commerce authority is created.
