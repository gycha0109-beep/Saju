3. genuinely new provenance required; aliases, republications, and retransmissions are not new provenance
4. exact visible-stem remote 克 force/applicability scope relevance required

6. primary or near-primary publication identity preferred over aggregator surfaces

## Frozen search channels

Six search channels are prospectively frozen:

1. institutional/library bibliographic traces for new authors and works
