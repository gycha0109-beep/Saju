# R139 — Methodology-composition admissibility matrix

Date: 2026-09-27  
Track: saju-research  
Status: research-only

## Purpose

R139 generalizes the multi-method boundaries established by R039, R040, R137, and R138 across all six methodology families:

- GEJU
- STRENGTH_FUIYI
- CLIMATE_TIAOHOU
- FLOW_TONGGUAN
- BINGYAO
- SPECIAL_FOLLOW

The matrix does not decide which method wins. It records whether evidence can coexist, whether semantic composition is conditionally representable, and whether composition remains parallel, unresolved, or explicitly non-composable in a bounded context.

## Three distinct gates

R139 keeps three questions separate:

1. Evidence coexistence: may method-specific evidence remain in the same research envelope?
2. Semantic composition: may the evidence be represented as one bounded composed interpretation?
3. Executable resolution: may an engine select, prioritize, collapse, or emit a final result?

The third gate remains closed for every R139 cell and probe.

## Shape

The six methodology families produce:

- 6 self pairs
- 15 unordered cross-method pairs
- 21 canonical pair cells total
- 36 directional projections
- 42 contextual probes, two per canonical pair

Pair keys are order-normalized only for identity. Canonical ordering is not precedence.

## Context dependence

A methodology pair does not determine one universal composition state. Each pair receives at least two different contexts. This prevents pair identity from becoming a hidden resolver.

The represented states are:

- SAME_METHOD_EVIDENCE_COEXISTENCE
- COEXISTENCE_ADMISSIBLE
- CONDITIONAL_COMPOSITION
- PARALLEL_PRESERVATION_ONLY
- COMPOSITION_UNRESOLVED
- NON_COMPOSITION_BOUNDARY
- METHOD_NOT_APPLICABLE
- INDETERMINATE

R139 uses only the states supported by the corpus; the type retains not-applicable and indeterminate for future bounded probes.

## Governed replays

All three R039 cases are replayed as COEXISTENCE_ADMISSIBLE:

- 丁巳 壬子 辛巳 丁酉 — strength support plus climate requirement
- 甲申 丙子 庚辰 甲申 — Geju structural use plus climate requirement
- 戊戌 甲子 己巳 戊辰 — Bingyao remedy relation plus climate requirement

These are coexistence evidence, not verified global composition rules and not method precedence.

## R137 and R138 preservation

R137 established that Tiaohou urgency does not create global precedence over structure and that synthetic contradiction is not source-verified true conflict.

R138 established that Tongguan and Bingyao are distinct resolution families and that their coexistence or synthetic opposition does not authorize a winner.

R139 therefore represents FLOW_TONGGUAN × BINGYAO as PARALLEL_PRESERVATION_ONLY or COMPOSITION_UNRESOLVED, never as a winner-selected composition.

## Special-pattern boundary

R134 and R135 prohibit treating ordinary-path damage as proof of special-pattern establishment.

Therefore every cross-method pair with SPECIAL_FOLLOW includes a NON_COMPOSITION_BOUNDARY baseline or an unresolved stress probe. Ordinary Geju, Fuyi, climate, Tongguan, or Bingyao evidence cannot automatically trigger a special-pattern transition.

## Same element, different role

R139 includes four adversarial probes where the same element value appears under two different methodology roles. Equality of element value does not imply equality of semantic role and does not prove composition.

## Rejected collapses

R139 rejects:

- method count as winner
- source count as winner
- same element as same role
- same element as composition proof
- array order as precedence
- methodology enum order as priority
- majority-vote method selection
- numeric method scoring
- weighted method scoring
- global method precedence
- automatic final Yongshen
- automatic role collapse
- automatic special-pattern transition
- automatic unresolved-conflict tie-break

## Authority

R139 is research-only. It does not authorize:

- a cross-method winner resolver
- a numeric or weighted priority system
- final Yongshen emission
- chart role fact emission
- special-pattern automatic transition
- automatic engine admission
- InterpretationClaim emission
- preview promotion
- production promotion

R140 is intentionally left to test preservation when composition is incompatible or unresolved. R139 must not absorb R140 by creating a generic cross-method resolver.
