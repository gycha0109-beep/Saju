# R143 — Branch combination-and-clash coexistence corpus

Date: 2026-09-27  
Track: saju-research  
Status: research-only

## Purpose

R143 studies coexistence between branch combination structures and canonical clash structures.

It does not define a global 合-versus-沖 precedence rule.

The corpus separates three evidence classes:

1. structural coexistence with no settlement authority
2. placement-sensitive three-combination settlement already authorized by I46
3. exact source-bounded interaction cases already preserved by R059

## 六合 coexistence surface

All six structural 六合 families are covered:

- 子丑
- 寅亥
- 卯戌
- 辰酉
- 巳申
- 午未

For each family R143 constructs three governed stress topologies:

- clash touches the left combination participant
- clash touches the right combination participant
- canonical clashes touch both participants

This produces 18 rows.

All 18 remain STRUCTURAL_COEXISTENCE_UNRESOLVED.

I43 confirms that 六合 structural participation remains valid while transformed-element adoption and transformation-state emission are blocked. It also explicitly leaves interaction settlement as a separate required policy.

Therefore R143 does not convert a structural 六合 candidate into either an effective combination or a no-effect result.

## Dual-clash boundary

Six rows contain two simultaneous canonical clashes.

The existence of two clashes is preserved as topology only.

R143 does not:

- add the clashes
- count them as stronger
- assign a numeric weight
- infer that the combination is broken
- infer that the combination survives

## 三合 placement-sensitive replay

All four canonical three-combination families are represented:

- 申子辰
- 巳酉丑
- 亥卯未
- 寅午戌

For each family R143 replays four I46 clash-placement classes:

- embedded within the bureau span and tight to the clashed participant
- embedded within the bureau span but not tight
- outside the bureau span but tight
- outside the bureau span and not tight

This produces 16 rows.

Only the first placement is source-bounded to emit BROKEN_BY_TIGHT_EMBEDDED_CLASH.

The two contextual placements remain CONTEXTUAL_INTACT_OR_DAMAGED_UNRESOLVED.

The outside-and-not-tight placement emits NO_DIRECT_SETTLEMENT_FROM_THIS_RULE. Absence of the direct break rule is not evidence of intactness.

## R059 direct replay

All six R059 direct conflict cases are replayed.

The direct corpus includes evidence in both directions:

- meeting resolving clash
- clash resolving meeting
- combination resolving clash
- clash resolving meeting
- resolution reactivating conflict
- a structural match that may be ineffective

These observations are source-bounded.

They cannot be collapsed into:

- combination always wins
- clash always wins
- meeting always wins
- one total relation order

## Corpus shape

R143 contains 40 rows:

- 18 六合 structural coexistence rows
- 16 三合 placement-boundary rows
- 6 R059 direct replay rows

No row authorizes generic precedence, multiple-clash aggregation, numeric relation weighting, transformed-element emission, or an executable generic settlement.

## Authority

R143 remains research-only.

It does not authorize:

- global combination/clash precedence
- generic clash-breaks-combination
- generic combination-resolves-clash
- multiple-clash aggregation
- numeric relation strength
- 六合 transformed-element emission
- generic post-interaction bureau state
- chart-role fact emission
