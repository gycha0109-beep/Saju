## Purpose

The Wealth Monthly authority review returned the current `0.1.0-research` candidate to traditional Saju Research without rejecting it.

The candidate is executable, exact-jeol-aware, and bounded, but its Wealth Monthly T9 interpretation semantics are grounded only in an internal policy source.
