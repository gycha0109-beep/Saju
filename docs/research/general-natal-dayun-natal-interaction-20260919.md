## Governing proposition

`命之格局，成於八字，然配之以運，亦有成格變格之要權`

The natal chart establishes the structure; luck interacts with that structure.

## Verified interaction classes

### 1. Completion of an incomplete natal structure
A natal structure can be `成而未全`; luck can supply the missing meeting/root/transparency condition and complete it.

### 2. Hidden natal material becoming operative
The commentary treats natal hidden material becoming transparent through luck as structurally significant.

### 3. Combination / meeting / clash changing structure
Luck can complete a meeting or introduce a clash that changes the structural configuration.

### 4. Natal blocking / rescue
`逢成格而不喜` and `逢變格而不忌` are both possible. Natal stems/branches can block, redirect, or rescue the apparent luck transition.

### 5. Ordinary help/harm vs pattern completion/change
The source explicitly distinguishes `行運助用害用` from rarer `成格變格` behavior.
