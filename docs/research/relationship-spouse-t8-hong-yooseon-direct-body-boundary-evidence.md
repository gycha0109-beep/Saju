## Positive evidence preserved

This article is important modern reinterpretation evidence.

1. It explicitly criticizes mechanically applying inherited family-star formulas across eras.
2. It recognizes that social change can make an inherited interpretation mismatched to lived family relations.
3. It does not merely criticize the old rule; it proposes a concrete reinterpretation criterion: the `의무`, `권리`, and `역할` imposed by a society's ideology.
4. Its conclusion explicitly treats ideology as an independent variable and the interpreted Yukchin Ten-God relation as something that can change with it.

Those findings must be preserved rather than reduced to “traditional sex rules only.”

## Why `EXPLICIT_ROLE_NEUTRAL_NATAL_MAPPING` remains OPEN
