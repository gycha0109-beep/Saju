# General Annual Research Return Handoff v1

Issue: #1523  
Track: `saju-bridge`  
Upstream Bridge review: #1512 / PR #1514

## Purpose

The General Annual authority review returned the current `0.1.0-research` candidate to traditional Saju Research without rejecting it.

The candidate is executable and bounded, but its current interpretation semantics are grounded only in `SRC-MYEONGHA-ANNUAL-INTERPRETATION-POLICY-V1`, an internal policy source. The current rules remain research-only with heuristic provenance, experimental methodology stability, and unreviewed reviewer status.

This handoff turns that `RETURN_TO_RESEARCH` decision into a deterministic, content-addressed Research contract. It does not perform source research, preserve the current semantic keys as truth, or admit any authority.

## Current candidate surface

The handoff binds the current General Annual candidate surface:

- candidate version: `0.1.0-research`;
- one interpretation pack;
- one methodology;
- ten annual-stem Ten-God activation rules;
- four annual-to-natal branch-clash tension rules;
- fourteen rules in total.

The candidate-surface hash covers the content-addressed pack, methodology, and sorted rule references.

The hash is a review binding, not a command for Research to preserve the current candidate. Research may narrow, change, split, or remove unsupported semantics. Any such candidate drift means a later Bridge review must use a fresh content-addressed review surface.

## Research workstream A — Annual stem Ten-God semantic authority

Research must establish what Annual-specific interpretation, if any, is source-qualified for a request-resolved annual stem Ten-God.

The current mappings such as `ANNUAL_PEER_SELF_DIRECTION` or `ANNUAL_WEALTH_STRUCTURED_RESOURCES` are candidate semantics, not research conclusions.

For each retained proposition family, Research must preserve:

- Source Statement;
- Interpretive Reading;
- Research Inference;
- scope and preconditions;
- allowed meaning and meaning strength;
- qualifiers and exceptions;
- counterexamples;
- school dependencies;
- non-implications;
- source and provenance references.

Research must not convert an Annual Ten-God into a luck score, deterministic event, wealth magnitude, health outcome, or relationship outcome.

## Research workstream B — Annual-to-natal branch-clash semantic authority

The temporal relation fact and its interpretation are separate.

Research must determine whether an annual-to-natal branch clash supports a bounded Annual interpretation and, if so, under which conditions and with what meaning strength.

The current candidate's `tension` narrative role and pillar-specific emphasis are not pre-approved authority. Research must verify, narrow, replace, or remove them according to the evidence.

A clash alone must not be promoted into an accident, illness, separation, financial loss, or other concrete deterministic event.

## Research workstream C — Annual scope and qualifier contract

Temporal authority scopes remain separate:

```text
Natal Authority != Annual Authority != Monthly Authority
```

Natal authority may be reused only after claim-level review demonstrates that the Annual application is supported. Natal semantics are not inherited wholesale.

Annual Research success does not authorize Monthly semantics.

Research must return explicit scope, required inputs, preconditions, allowed conclusions, qualifiers, exceptions, counterexamples, school dependencies, prohibited extensions, and unresolved questions.

## Research workstream D — Product-policy semantic separation

`SRC-MYEONGHA-ANNUAL-INTERPRETATION-POLICY-V1` may remain useful for product/request boundaries such as:

- target-year request handling;
- packaging and narrative constraints;
- conservative forbidden-inference policy;
- consumer-side request scope.

It is not standalone traditional Saju semantic authority.

Reading Profile coverage, executable fixtures, and temporal fact derivation likewise do not establish interpretation authority.
