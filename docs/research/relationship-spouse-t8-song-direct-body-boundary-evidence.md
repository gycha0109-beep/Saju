## The thesis's actual modern proposal

The proposal is a **meta-rule for selecting which inherited Yukchin system to use**, not one new Yukchin relationship mapping.

The body recommends, in substance:

```text
sex or genealogy-centered relationship question
  -> Yeonhaejapyeong-centered interpretation

actual role / actual influence-centered relationship question
  -> Jeokcheonsu or Gungtongbogam may be more appropriate in specific situations
```

It further gives household-context examples such as some married-woman or one-person-household cases for the Jeokcheonsu branch, and some rural / child-economic-provider households for the Gungtongbogam branch.

The conclusion repeats that selection logic and says the systems should be mixed according to the situation rather than applied uniformly.
