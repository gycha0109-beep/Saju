## Claim classes

### C1 — configuration-level official/status interpretation
官格、財生官、官印等 configurations are discussed in status/貴 and office-bearing contexts.

Boundary: the claim is configuration-level, not `官 present -> career X`.

### C2 — historical public-office examples
The commentary supplies complete-chart examples tied to historically specific office/rank outcomes.

Boundary: examples are case evidence, not a universal job classifier.

### C3 — role-specific existing-official context
三命通會 includes claims such as:
- 官員以月為僚友
- 官員以時為帝座禍福

These are explicitly scoped to people already read in an official-role context.

### C4 — kin official/status claims
三命通會 also contains:
- 年上官星，父祖為官
- 月上官星，兄弟必貴

These concern relatives and must not be reinterpreted as the native's occupation.

## Modern-transfer boundary

The selected corpus does not directly establish:
- 正官 -> government employee
- 七煞 -> military / police / executive
- 食傷 -> creative profession
- 財 -> finance / business profession
- 印 -> education / research profession
- 比劫 -> entrepreneurship / sales

Those modern occupation mappings require separate contemporary evidence and explicit translation methodology.
