# General Monthly Research Return Handoff v1

Issue: #1719  
Track: `saju-bridge`  
Upstream Bridge review: #1531 / PR #1532

## Purpose

The General Monthly authority review returned the current `0.1.0-research` candidate to traditional Saju Research without rejecting it.

The candidate is executable, exact-jeol-aware, and bounded, but its current interpretation semantics are grounded only in the internal `SRC-MYEONGHA-MONTHLY-INTERPRETATION-POLICY-V1` policy source. Its rules remain research-only, heuristic, experimental, and unreviewed.

This handoff turns that `RETURN_TO_RESEARCH` decision into a deterministic, content-addressed Research contract. It does not perform source research, preserve current Monthly semantic keys as truth, or admit authority.

## Current candidate surface

The handoff binds:

- candidate version: `0.1.0-research`;
- one interpretation pack;
- one methodology;
- two civil-month segments: `before_jeol` and `after_jeol`;
- twenty segment stem Ten-God activation rules;
- eight segment-to-natal branch-clash tension rules;
- twenty-eight rules total.

The candidate-surface hash covers the content-addressed pack, methodology, and sorted rule references.

Candidate drift is allowed. Research may narrow, change, split, or remove unsupported semantics. Any such drift requires a later Bridge review to bind a fresh content-addressed surface.

## Research workstream A — Monthly segment stem Ten-God semantic authority

Research must establish what Monthly-specific interpretation, if any, is source-qualified for a resolved monthly-segment stem Ten-God.

Current semantic keys are candidate semantics only.

For each retained proposition family, Research must preserve:

- Source Statement;
- Interpretive Reading;
- Research Inference;
- scope and preconditions;
- allowed meaning and meaning strength;
- qualifiers and exceptions;
- counterexamples;
- school dependencies;
- non-implications;
- source and provenance references.

A Monthly Ten-God must not become a luck score or deterministic event guarantee.

## Research workstream B — Segment-to-natal branch-clash semantic authority

The resolved segment branch relation and its interpretation are separate.

Research must determine whether a segment-to-natal branch clash supports a bounded Monthly interpretation and, if so, under which conditions and with what meaning strength.

Clash alone must not be promoted into accident, illness, separation, financial loss, or another concrete deterministic event.

## Research workstream C — Segment and pillar emphasis authority

The current candidate distinguishes:

```text
after_jeol  = major
before_jeol = moderate
```

and applies pillar-specific emphasis to clash tension.

These are not pre-approved authority.

Research must verify, narrow, replace, or remove these emphasis rules. Exact solar-term segmentation does not itself establish a difference in interpretive meaning strength.

## Research workstream D — Monthly scope and qualifier contract

Temporal authority scopes remain distinct:

```text
Natal Authority != Annual Authority != Monthly Authority
```

Natal or Annual authority may be reused only after claim-level review establishes that the Monthly application is supported. Neither is inherited wholesale.

Research must return explicit scope, required inputs, preconditions, allowed conclusions, qualifiers, exceptions, counterexamples, school dependencies, prohibited extensions, and unresolved questions.

## Research workstream E — Product-policy / segmentation / semantics separation

Exact jeol-aware segmentation is valid temporal-input capability.

`SRC-MYEONGHA-MONTHLY-INTERPRETATION-POLICY-V1` may remain useful for request packaging, conservative safety boundaries, and product behavior.

Neither temporal precision nor internal product policy is standalone traditional Saju semantic authority.

Likewise, executable fixtures and Reading Profile coverage do not establish interpretation authority.
