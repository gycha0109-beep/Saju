## Allowed conclusion

The current repository supports only these conclusions:

1. the Career Annual candidate is technically representable and executable as research;
2. annual temporal facts can provide bounded input evidence;
3. Career Natal research claims can coexist in the pack without conferring Career Annual authority;
4. one bounded Natal Position authority component exists but remains Natal-only;
5. the candidate explicitly blocks deterministic employment and career-outcome overreach;
6. Career Annual-specific interpretation authority is not yet established.

The Bridge therefore does not rewrite the candidate. It returns the Career Annual semantic authority gap to Research.

## Research return

Research must establish, with source-qualified evidence:

1. the admissible meaning and strength of Career Annual stem Ten-God working-pattern semantics;
2. the admissible meaning and strength of Annual-to-natal branch-clash work-adjustment semantics, if retained;
3. whether the current Career axes, semantic keys, adjustment areas, activation emphasis, and pillar-specific clash emphasis have source support or must be narrowed/removed;
4. Career Annual-specific scope, qualifiers, exceptions, counterexamples, school dependencies, and non-implications;
5. whether any Career Natal or General Annual authority is reusable claim-by-claim;
6. a clean separation between product/narrative policy and traditional Saju semantic authority.

Research must not convert these candidate semantics into hiring, firing, resignation, job-change, promotion, compensation, business-success, or other deterministic career-event predictions.

## Later governance

Source closure is not enough for Engine admission.

After Career Annual source authority is established, later work must still separately establish:

- a content-addressed review surface for the exact admissible Career Annual subjects;
- real domain ReviewAttestations;
- independent ReviewerTrustGrants;
- governed provenance-quality review;
- governed lifecycle-promotion review;
- a new Bridge admission decision.

No gate cascades automatically into the next one.

## Prohibited shortcuts

The review explicitly forbids:

- Career Natal → Career Annual automatic authority inheritance;
- General Annual → Career Annual automatic authority inheritance;
- Career Annual → Career Monthly automatic authority expansion;
- bounded Natal Position component → Annual generalization;
- internal Career Annual product policy → traditional Saju semantic authority;
- executable code, fixtures, narrative copy, or Reading Profile coverage → authority;
- current Career axes, adjustment areas, or emphasis → authority without source support;
- hiring, firing, resignation, job-change, promotion, compensation, or business-success prediction;
- fabricated review/trust authority;
- automatic Research lifecycle promotion;
- Engine, Preview, Official Reading, or Production promotion from this review.
