## R101–R200 design

These are **candidate research frontiers**, not automatically authorized implementation tasks.

Design constraints:

- research-only; not a product or commerce backlog;
- existing open R001–R100 work remains open under its original identity rather than being falsely renumbered as complete;
- source/work/edition/witness provenance remains explicit;
- calculation convention and authority remain explicit;
- no numeric hidden strength or confidence score;
- school/source divergence remains representable;
- counterexamples and falsification are first-class;
- reproducibility and temporal sensitivity are first-class;
- modern-domain semantic bridges must preserve source scope;
- validation includes adversarial and perturbation work;
- evidence infrastructure is tested by population/replay, not only by schema existence;
- no frontier promotes Production authority by appearing in this list.
