## Source evidence

Selected `子平真詮評注 / 論雜氣如何取用` commentary provides two placement anchors.

### Year / hour placement

The commentary first speaks of two stems as transparent and then describes a placement where they are distributed across year and hour:

```text
甲生辰月，壬戊財印兩透，如財印分居年時
```

This is sufficient to establish **positive source-example usage** of year and hour stem positions for transparency in the selected mixed-qi discussion.

It does **not** establish that year and hour are the only valid positions.

### Month placement

The same commentary gives:

```text
如甲生辰月，而為丙年壬辰月
...
蓋壬透自辰
```

`壬辰月` places `壬` in the month-stem position, while the commentary explicitly describes that `壬` as transparent from `辰`.

This is sufficient to establish **positive source-example usage** of the month stem position.

It does **not** establish that the month stem is mandatory or exclusive.
