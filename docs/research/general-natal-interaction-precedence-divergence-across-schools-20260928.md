# R148 — Interaction precedence divergence across schools

Date: 2026-09-28  
Track: saju-research  
Status: research-only

## Purpose

R148 records where stem/branch interaction precedence appears source-bounded, context-dependent, placement-sensitive, or unresolved.

It does not choose a canonical school and does not create a global precedence ladder.

The title says "across schools", but the governed corpus does not establish a verified school-lineage divergence for every interaction source. R148 therefore separates:

1. source/method/context divergence
2. proposition divergence
3. verified school-lineage divergence

Only the first two are observed here. Verified school-lineage divergence remains unestablished.

## Evidence shape

R148 contains 16 precedence-evidence rows:

- 6 R059 direct interaction cases
- 6 R143 six-combination structural controls
- 4 I46 placement-policy rows

It also contains:

- 5 proposition syntheses
- 6 governance guards

## Five propositions

R148 tracks five propositions without authorizing any as a global rule:

- P1 — combination over clash
- P2 — clash over combination
- P3 — meeting over clash
- P4 — clash over meeting
- P5 — clash breaks a three-combination bureau

### P1 combination over clash

R059 contains a bounded combination→clash RESOLVES case.

It also contains a combination→clash CAN_REACTIVATE case, while all six R143 六合 families remain structurally coexistent and unsettled under clash stress.

Therefore bounded support exists, but stable or generic dominance does not.

### P2 clash over combination

R059 contains a clash→combination MAY_BE_INEFFECTIVE case.

The six 六合 structural controls remain unresolved.

The I46 tight embedded rule is not imported here because it belongs to a narrower three-combination bureau proposition.

Therefore generic clash-over-combination support remains insufficient.

### P3 and P4 meeting versus clash

R059 directly preserves both directions:

- meeting resolves clash
- clash resolves meeting

Both bounded observations remain valid simultaneously.

Their coexistence directly blocks a single global meeting-versus-clash ordering.

Case count is not a vote: two clash→meeting cases do not outweigh one meeting→clash case.

### P5 clash breaks three-combination bureau

I46 allows exactly one deterministic placement:

- clash counterpart embedded within the bureau span
- tightly adjacent to the bureau participant it clashes

The other tracked placements remain contextual or emit no direct settlement.

Therefore the proposition is placement-sensitive and source-bounded.

It is not a generic clash-wins rule.

## Six-combination controls

R143 contains three stress topologies for each 六合 family:

- clash touches left participant
- clash touches right participant
- clashes touch both participants

R148 aggregates these into six family-level controls.

Every family remains STRUCTURAL_COEXISTENCE_UNRESOLVED.

This supports neither:

- combination always wins
- clash always wins

## School-lineage boundary

R095 requires lineage assertions and proposition comparisons to remain separate.

R148 therefore does not infer school divergence merely because different source labels or work families appear.

Every evidence row keeps:

- lineageAssertionState = INCONCLUSIVE
- schoolDivergenceEstablished = false

The supported conclusion is narrower:

> source/method/context-level precedence divergence is observed.

A verified cross-school proposition divergence would require separate lineage evidence and explicit proposition comparison.
