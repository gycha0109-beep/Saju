## Direct semantic classes

徐樂吾 commentary explicitly states that 財 can denote:
- 財帛
- 妻妾
- 才能
- 驛馬

These are related historical categories, not one literal-money token.

## Outcome conditions

The same source makes beneficial wealth interpretation conditional:

- `財為我剋，必須身強，萬能剋制`
- if the native is weak, 財 may become harmful rather than beneficial;
- 財格 success is configuration-dependent, including 財生官, 食生財 with strength support, and 財印 placement;
- `財多身弱` does not authorize a rich interpretation.

Therefore:

- WEALTH_STAR_PRESENT != RICH
- MORE_WEALTH != MORE_RICH
- WEALTH_STRONG != GUARANTEED_RICH

## Rejected modern shortcuts

- 正財 -> salary / stable income
- 偏財 -> investing / speculation / entrepreneurship
- 財 -> literal money in every context
- 財多 -> high net worth
- 財旺 -> high income
- 財格成 -> guaranteed financial success

Those modern mappings require separate evidence and translation rules.
