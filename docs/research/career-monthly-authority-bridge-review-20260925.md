## Allowed conclusion

The current repository supports only these conclusions:

1. the Career Monthly candidate is technically representable and executable as research;
2. the temporal layer can preserve exact solar-term-aware before/after-jeol facts;
3. Career Natal research claims can coexist in the pack without conferring Career Monthly authority;

Research must not convert these candidate semantics into hiring, firing, resignation, job-change, promotion, compensation, business-success, or other deterministic career-event predictions.
