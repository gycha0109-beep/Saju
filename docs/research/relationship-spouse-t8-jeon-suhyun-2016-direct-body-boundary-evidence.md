### Printed p.100 / physical PDF 110

The thesis's own modernization section, `官星論의 현대적 조명`, gives the decisive modern semantic assignment.

```text

men        → children
```

The source therefore demonstrates that sex-common semantics are available for the social/workplace dimension while kinship semantics remain explicitly conditioned on native sex.

### Printed pp.103-104 / physical PDF 113-114

The subsequent research-task section calls for stronger objective cases and documentary/logical support to validate occupational divisions and spouse/child roles in more detail.

That is a call for better validation of existing relationship-role interpretation, not a published replacement spouse selector.

This repetition after the modernization section removes the possibility of reading the earlier sex-conditioned mapping as a discarded legacy rule. The thesis ends while retaining it.
