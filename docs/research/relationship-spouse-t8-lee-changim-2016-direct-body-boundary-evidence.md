## Render-first direct-body review

All 84 physical PDF pages were rendered before semantic judgment. Text extraction was used only to locate material passages.

### Physical 20 / printed 11

The inherited palace-theory discussion still frames the Day Branch in male/wife terms. It states that for a man the Day Branch is wife palace / Wealth context and discusses favorable or unfavorable wife outcomes through that frame.

This is not a role-neutral spouse-star rule.

### Physical 21 / printed 12

The thesis explicitly defines the Yukshin compatibility spouse-star layer:

```text
female native -> Officer / 官星 = husband star / 夫星
male native   -> Wealth / 財星 = wife star / 妻星
```

Those spouse stars are then judged by their generating/combining or clash/punishment/harm relation to the counterpart chart and by connection to the Day-Branch spouse palace.

This is direct body evidence that the source retains native-sex-conditioned spouse-star semantics.

### Physical 24 / printed 15

The wider compatibility theory remains explicitly dyadic and male/female framed. The Yongshin section compares the man's Yongshin with the woman's Yongshin, and the cited pairing rule matches Peer-heavy men with Output-heavy women and the converse female branch.

The complete inherited framework is therefore not a single-native sex-neutral selector.

### Physical 43 / printed 34

The thesis's own empirical method operationalizes the selected Gungwi layer. Interview material about couples' outer and inner compatibility is grouped and analyzed by Day Stem and Day Branch:

```text
Day Stem / 日干   -> outer compatibility / 겉궁합
Day Branch / 日支 -> inner compatibility / 속궁합
```

The author says generating, same-element, or combining Day-Stem relations correspond to emotional stability, while Day Branch is used for the inner/physical compatibility layer.

This is genuine operational evidence.

### Physical 44 / printed 35

The first result class is explicitly:

```text
both partners' Day Stems = generating / same-element / combining
both partners' Day Branches = generating / same-element / combining
```

The concrete case is presented as separate `乾` and `坤` charts. The operational method therefore consumes two partner natal charts and compares them.

### Physical 72-73 / printed 63-64

The conclusion summarizes four operational classes formed by the two partners' Day-Stem and Day-Branch relation states. This confirms that the selected Gungwi method is not merely descriptive background: it is the thesis's applied compatibility classification method.

But its input unit remains the couple. It does not select or map a spouse from one native's natal facts.

### Physical 77 / printed 68

The author explicitly states that the study selected **Gungwi theory only** from a broader Japyunginjeon compatibility system that contains Gungwi, Yukshin, and Yongshin because combining all three would be broad and difficult to adjudicate. The same limitation section explicitly says the study's unit was the married couple and calls for future comparison with divorced couples.

No replacement single-native spouse selector is supplied in the limitation or conclusion.
