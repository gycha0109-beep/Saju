**Source**

- 송재우, `명리학 육친론 비교연구 : (연해자평, 적천수, 궁통보감을 중심으로)`


The source instead exposes external/contextual inputs and an explicit husband-criterion gap.

## Candidate 3 — 홍유선 2022

**Source**

- 홍유선, `이데올로기적 접근을 통한 육친 간 상극관계 해석: 부부, 부자, 고부를 중심으로`
- `문화·경영·기술` 2(2), 75-89
- KCI `ART003089059`
- RISS `A108419413`
- DOI `10.54385/cbt.2022.2.2.75`

This is the strongest public scholarly challenge found so far to treating traditional gendered Yukchin assignment as timeless natural semantics.

The abstract says that the traditional `wife = Wealth` relation is explained by patriarchal obligations imposed after marriage, including producing a son and supporting parents-in-law. Under that ideological structure, the husband can be interpreted as `Officer` because he controls the wife. The paper's stated goal is to provide a criterion for resetting Yukchin Ten-God meanings in changing society, using `duty` and `role` imposed by social ideology.

That materially changes the frontier:

```text
traditional wife = Wealth
is treated as historically/socially contingent
rather than automatically timeless natural semantics
```

But it still does not establish the production-ready replacement required here.

The public surface requires semantic context such as:

```text
- actual relationship role
- socially imposed duty
- applicable ideology
- direction of obligation/control
```

Those are external social facts, not facts derivable from `CanonicalSajuSnapshot`. The abstract also still explains its spouse examples in `wife / husband` terms and does not expose an executable partner-neutral natal selector.

Therefore:

