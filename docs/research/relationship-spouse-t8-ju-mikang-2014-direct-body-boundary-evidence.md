## Render-first direct-body review

### Physical p.52 / printed p.38 — spouse-star selector is explicitly sex-conditioned

Under `육신(六神)에 의한 궁합론`, the thesis focuses on spouse-star 生/合. It directly defines the operational branch as:

```text
female native -> 官星 = 夫星
male native   -> 財星 = 妻星
```

The source then judges those spouse stars against the partner by favorable 生/合 or unfavorable 충·극·형·파·해 and treats their rooting in the spouse-palace Day Branch as favorable. This is executable spouse-specific evidence, but it is not native-sex-independent or partner-sex-independent.

### Physical p.75 / printed p.61 — integrated three-layer method and two-chart execution

The thesis synthesizes the three perspectives and defines the ideal compatibility condition through all of them together:

- palace: male/female Day Pillars form favorable relations without specified harms;
- Yukshin: the male/female spouse stars 官星 and 財星 are in the spouse-palace Day Branch;
- Yongshin: the relevant elements also function as Yongshin.

It then analyzes a concrete male chart born 1976-06-15 22:00 and female chart born 1984-04-09 16:00 side by side. The complete operational method is therefore dyadic rather than a single-native natal-only selector.

### Physical p.80 / printed p.66 — executable sex-conditioned partner-selection rule remains in the conclusion

The conclusion restates the historic operational rule that a man with much 比肩·劫財 should choose a woman with much 食神·傷官, and conversely a woman with much 食神·傷官 should choose a man with much 比肩·劫財. The thesis discusses later criticism of this rule, but its method history and synthesis remain explicitly male/female partitioned rather than replacing spouse selection with one role-neutral rule.

### Physical p.81 / printed p.67 — spouse stars remain 官/財 and complete judgment includes fortune flow

The conclusion again states the ideal condition using `남녀의 배우자성인 관성과 재성` in each spouse-palace Day Branch. It then cautions that compatibility must not be treated as fixed and explicitly says that Saju judgment values not only the natal chart but also `대운과 세운`, so fortune flow should be considered in compatibility.

This is a second independent blocker to the requested natal-facts-only complete input contract.

### Physical p.82 / printed p.68 — modernization is real but does not publish a replacement selector

The thesis recognizes modern changes in knowledge society, economic independence, and relationship-role expectations. It contrasts older female `官印相生` preference with newer female `食傷生財 / 財生官` and male `食傷生財` structures, and says that Yin/Yang Day-Stem matching increasingly need not distinguish men and women.

That modernization tension is preserved. It does not erase the source's earlier `female 官=夫 / male 財=妻` spouse-star rule or provide an executable replacement spouse selector independent of native sex and partner sex.
