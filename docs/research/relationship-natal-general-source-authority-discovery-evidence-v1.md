## Why no rule is admitted yet

The strongest sources found so far support one of three weaker propositions:

1. Ten Gods can be used as relationship-oriented/interpersonal symbols;
2. modern relationship contexts require reinterpretation;
3. specific low-provenance modern sites propose individual relationship traits.

None establishes the exact current rule contract with sufficient provenance.
