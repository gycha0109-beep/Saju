## Explicit candidate failures

### Song Sang-seop 2022

The public scholarly surface is already sufficient to establish that the candidate is **not** an explicit role-neutral spouse mapping source. It describes spouse allocations in husband/wife and male/female terms, including Wealth for wife and Officer for husband, and separately discusses Ren Tieqiao-lineage Direct/Indirect Wealth wife/concubine allocation.

This material may remain useful for historical methodology provenance. It cannot be silently universalized.

### Eum Jong-hee 2019

The abstract states that `財星` means wife and wealth. It also makes the operational method depend on Yin-Yang-specific action and on strength, circulation, and role.

Two independent boundaries therefore remain:

1. the spouse applicability is explicitly wife-specific;
2. the method requires semantic inputs not currently governed merely by having raw stems/branches or Ten-God labels.

### Yoon Sangheum 2023

The title and English title explicitly say the study focuses on Wealth and Officer **in male charts**. The article is useful evidence on historical gender-role controversy, but its explicit scope cannot be generalized into a role-neutral natal spouse rule.

## Park Oe-suk 2023 is a distinct methodology, not a shortcut

Park's abstract is methodologically significant because it argues that Saju Yukchin is broader than modern Sipsin/Sipseong and includes:

- kin relationships formed through Yin-Yang union;
- Yin-Yang-divided Five-Element relations;
- positional palace meaning.

However, the public National Assembly table list separately exposes:

```text
남명육친취용도
여명육친취용도
육친의 기본 의미와 상호 관계적 의미-남자 기준
관성이 없는 여자의 남편(坤命)
궁위와 육친의 활용(乾命)
```

That is evidence of a gender-split analytical presentation, not evidence of a single role-neutral spouse selector.

The repository must also **not** collapse this broader Yukchin framework into the existing `derivedFacts.tenGods` semantics merely because both use related vocabulary.

## Shin Jae-eok 2024 remains body-dependent

Shin's abstract materially establishes that:

- classical sources disagree in Yukchin assignments and operations;
- a Yukchin palace may be fixed while the corresponding Yukchin Star is absent;
- another Five Element may operate in its place in the examined cases.

That makes the thesis relevant to the current semantic-correspondence problem, but the case-change mechanism cannot be generalized into a spouse rule without direct body context and method constraints.

## Jung Su-a 2025 becomes the second acquisition candidate

Jung's 2025 thesis is newer and explicitly compares classical and modern Gungseong interpretation. Its abstract recasts major Ten-God families into modern domains such as economic situation, social recognition, interpersonal connections, occupational authority, academic achievement, and mentorship.

That modern vocabulary does **not** itself establish spouse neutrality. Nevertheless, it is now the most useful follow-up acquisition target after Kim because the thesis explicitly investigates how Gungseong theory changes in modern practical interpretation and contains dedicated case chapters for `財星` and `官星`.
