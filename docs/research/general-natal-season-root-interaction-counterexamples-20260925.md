## 1. Research question

R122 does not attempt to produce a final 身強身弱 / 旺衰 classifier.

It asks a narrower falsification question:

> Which simplistic season × root heuristics are already contradicted, bounded, or left unresolved by the governed R012–R020 research corpus?

The target is not a positive scoring formula. The target is a structured registry of failed shortcuts.

## 2. Evidence boundary

R122 is a repository synthesis task.

It reuses already-governed R012–R020 evidence and R121's post-R011 dependency map.

No new classical-source authority is created by this artifact.

Each case keeps three layers separate:

```text
Source Statement
!= Interpretive Reading
!= Research Inference
```

The corpus must not silently turn an interpretive reading into a source quotation or a research inference into a governed rule.

## 15. What R122 establishes

R122 establishes a reusable falsification corpus showing that:

1. season alone is insufficient for final strength;
2. root alone is insufficient for final strength;
3. visible repeated support cannot automatically substitute for root;
4. month importance is scoped and non-exclusive;
5. Tougan and Tonggen are not one predicate;
6. Yin/Yang and source-stratum divergences must remain visible;
7. temporal variability must not be silently converted into weights;
8. repeated support lacks a governed aggregation threshold;
9. textual overlap is not independent corroboration by default.
