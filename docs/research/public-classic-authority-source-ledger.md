## 1. Boundary

This ledger records publicly accessible witnesses that may support a genuinely new, non-equivalent methodology frontier after I249. It must not be used to:

- treat a later web transcription as proof of an earlier source,
- infer same-work identity from author/title similarity without primary bibliographic confirmation,
- close the existing Sohu-2017 authorship/lineage gap by similarity,
- infer numeric weights from qualitative wording,
- infer effective interaction from mere co-presence,
- convert source availability into production authority,
- pool non-independent witnesses as if they were independent authorities.

Existing terminal holds remain intact unless a later governed evidence-ingestion gate explicitly closes their frozen requirements.

**Source class**: National Library of China physical-book scan mirrored on Wikimedia Commons.

Earlier NLC variant retained for comparison:

- `nlc:data_416,17jh007058,102955` — 1934 `韋千里命學講義`

The NLC catalog for the 1936 witness explicitly lists 卷五 `補充篇` and `評斷篇`, matching the theoretical stream previously misbound to the 1935 `千里命稿` witness.

#### Exact primary-page bindings established by I253

**Printed p.14 / PDF page 301** directly contains:

- `明暗`, including `透干為明。藏支為暗`;
- `地位`, including an intervening-pillar separation example;
- `支冲`, beginning with the six-position clash definition and directly describing `子中癸水` overcoming `午中丁火` under `子午相冲`.

This primary page revalidates, under the corrected work identity:

- visible/hidden distinction;
- qualitative position/separation context;
- explicit relation-local branch-clash hidden-stem interaction.

**Not yet primary-page rebound**:

- `本氣 / 時令 / 多寡` continuation. The public transcription sequence aligns these immediately after the p.14 `支冲` opening, but the continuation page itself has not yet been successfully rendered and directly inspected. Transcription alignment is a locator aid only and may not substitute for primary page binding.

Therefore the old I252 four-class adequacy statement is not currently fully revalidated under corrected provenance. Three direct classes are primary-page rebound; the season/plurality qualifier class is held pending exact continuation-page inspection.

#### Career candidate discovered on the corrected witness

**Printed p.49 / PDF page 336**, section `事業`, directly includes named Ten-God relation patterns tied to historical work meanings, including:

- `殺印相生` → `宜武備`;
- `傷食生財` → `宜貿遷`.

This is recorded only as a page-bound historical Career relation **candidate** for later personalized Career T8 requirement-coverage review.

It does **not** authorize:

- automatic modernization of `武備`, `貿遷`, or other historical occupation labels;
- a universal Ten-God-to-career mapping;
- direct Career T8 rule authoring;
- authority admission or gap closure;
- numeric weighting, winner selection, damage magnitude, or post-relation settlement.

The public CText stream may remain useful for text discovery and variant comparison, but it is not itself promoted to primary-witness status and may not fill a missing primary page.
