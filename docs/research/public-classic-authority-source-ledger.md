## 2. NLC / Wikimedia Commons primary-scan candidates

### 2.1 韋千里《千里命稿》 — 1935 NLC scan

**Source class**: National Library of China physical-book scan mirrored on Wikimedia Commons.  
**Author**: 韋千里  
**Publisher**: 韋氏命苑  
**Publication**: 民國24年 / 1935  
**Witness**: 123-page PDF, NLC batch identity `nlc:data_416,01jh000372,10197`.

Locators:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC416-01jh000372-10197_%E5%8D%83%E9%87%8C%E5%91%BD%E7%A8%BF.pdf
- https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=497083&if=en

Directly observed candidate rule-bearing text in the CText transcription of the same work includes:

- `明暗`: visible stem versus hidden branch distinction.
- `地位`: a relationship separated by an intervening pillar is described as difficult/weak.
- `支冲`: branch clash is explained through hidden stems mutually overcoming one another.
- `本气 / 时令 / 多寡`: branch-clash outcome is qualified by main qi, season, and plurality.
- examples for `子午冲`, `寅申冲`, `辰戌丑未冲` explicitly discuss hidden-stem overcoming.

**Potential support**:

- hidden-stem interaction can be rule-bearing under an explicit branch-clash relation,
- interaction is context-sensitive rather than mere hidden-stem co-presence,
- position/separation can qualify interaction,
- season and plurality can qualify a clash outcome.

**Does NOT by itself support**:

- a universal hidden-stem-to-hidden-stem interaction rule outside an explicit relation,
- translating qualitative terms into numeric weights,
- importing these rules directly into Career T8,
- proving the provenance or authorship of the 2017 Sohu hidden-stem candidate.

Additional edition witnesses located and retained for variant comparison only:

- 1935 NLC copy: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC416-17jh002565-109431_%E5%8D%83%E9%87%8C%E5%91%BD%E7%A8%BF.pdf
- 1938 NLC copy: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC416-13jh001789-59640_%E5%8D%83%E9%87%8C%E5%91%BD%E7%A8%BF.pdf
