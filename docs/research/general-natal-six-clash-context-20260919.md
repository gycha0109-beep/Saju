## Structural pair identity

The six opposite-branch pairs are structurally recognized:

```text
子午
丑未
寅申
卯酉
辰戌
巳亥
```

Structural identity is not contextual effect.

## Direct contextual axes

The commentary explicitly makes clash effect depend on more than pair presence.

### Position / distance

```text
沖者，剋也
貼近為剋
遙動為沖
```

This establishes position as material, not a numeric distance score.

### Competing relations

```text
會合可以解刑沖
刑沖亦可以解會合
須看地位與性質
無一定之方式
```

A clash may be resolved, may resolve another relation, or may be reintroduced by a different relation.

### Role / polarity context

The source explicitly rejects `clash = always bad`.

A clash against a useful/favored component can be damaging; a clash that removes an excessive or adverse component can be useful.

### Branch-category variation

The commentary distinguishes:
- 寅申巳亥: potentially severe because they are 生地;
- 子午卯酉: outcome depends on the configuration;
- 辰戌丑未: same-Earth clashes are treated differently and often with less direct damage.

This is a qualitative source distinction only.

### Multiplicity / activation

Examples discuss one-vs-multiple competing branches and reactivation by an arriving branch, but the commentary itself warns against turning those examples into a rigid universal count rule.
