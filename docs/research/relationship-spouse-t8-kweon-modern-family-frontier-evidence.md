## Material positive signal

The authored abstract explicitly treats changing family forms as a reason to reinterpret traditional Ten-God relationship semantics. Among the examples it expressly includes:

- non-marriage families;
- one-person families;
- Miss Mom families;
- homosexual cohabitation families;
- companion-animal families.

Most importantly for the spouse frontier, the same abstract says that marriage interpretation may be derived from both Ten-God theory and palace theory and states that a homosexual cohabitation family leaves room for interpretation through the `日支`, described there as the spouse palace and the native's own base.

This matters because earlier modern sources either:

1. criticized traditional husband/wife assignment while requiring external ideology/duty/role context; or
2. mentioned actual-role interpretation but explicitly lacked a complete husband criterion; or
3. exposed a spouse-palace method whose executable judgment required ungoverned 格局/用神/喜神/忌神 states.

Kweon is stronger on one narrow point: the same source places a non-traditional same-sex family form next to an explicit Day-Branch spouse-palace interpretive possibility.

- whether any additional constructs such as 格局, 用神, strength, 旺衰, root, 十二運星, or other ungoverned states enter the actual method.

The phrase-level positive signal therefore cannot be promoted into a complete production rule.
