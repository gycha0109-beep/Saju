## 1. Research question

R153 established that an annual state must retain annual stem and branch participation while composing with natal and Dayun context, including meeting/combination and punishment/clash checks.

R154 asks a narrower follow-up question:

> Does the order in which natal, Dayun, annual, and cross-layer relation checks are enumerated or evaluated create semantic precedence?

The research answer encoded here is **no**.

The corpus does not deny that a future source-bounded methodology may define a meaningful precedence relation. It establishes only that the current governed assets do not authorize deriving precedence from data order, evaluation order, first match, last application, or temporal-layer recency.
