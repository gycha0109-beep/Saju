### Kim Mi-kyung 2026 — KCI candidate

`명리학(命理學)에서 오행·십성(十星) 간 융합에 의한인성(人性) 고찰`

`명리 십신의 관계변화와 재해석 : 현대 가족관계와 사회관계를 중심으로`

The thesis supports using Ten Gods as a family/social relationship framework while simultaneously arguing that modern relationship structures require explicit reinterpretation.

Therefore historical role mappings cannot be silently reused as modern general-relationship rules.
