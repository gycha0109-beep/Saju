## Direct source surfaces

### 徐樂吾《太歲》
`至本年每月之吉凶，倣此推究`

The instruction follows an annual framework that first composes natal chart, Dayun, annual stem/branch, and their interactions. Monthly interpretation is therefore presented as a lower-layer application by analogy, not as a replacement for the higher layers.

### 淵海子平 chronology examples
The text contains bounded cases where a specific month participates inside an already established luck/year configuration.

## Boundary model

MONTHLY_LAYER requires unresolved upstream context:
1. NATAL_CONTEXT

3. ANNUAL_CONTEXT

5. CROSS_LAYER_INTERACTIONS

R075 does not authorize a monthly result from the month pillar alone.
