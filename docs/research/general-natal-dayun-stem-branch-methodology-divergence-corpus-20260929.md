# R152 — Dayun stem/branch methodology divergence corpus

Date: 2026-09-29  
Track: saju-research  
Status: research-only

## Purpose

R152 preserves the methodological variance already present in R071 without converting it into a synthetic percentage formula or a canonical school choice.

The corpus answers:

> How should distinct Dayun stem/branch propositions coexist when their operational reconciliation is not yet authorized?

## Source basis

R152 replays exactly the four R071 propositions:

1. 子平之法，大運看支，歲君看干
   - DAYUN_BRANCH_EMPHASIS
2. 凡行運在干，兼用地支之神
   - STEM_PERIOD_WITH_BRANCH_PARTICIPATION
3. 在支則棄天干之物。蓋大運重地支
   - BRANCH_PERIOD_STEM_DISCARD_IN_ONE_FORMULATION
4. 運行十載數，上下五年分
   - TEN_YEAR_UPPER_LOWER_FIVE_YEAR_SPLIT

R152 does not add a numeric interpretation to any of these statements.

## Corpus shape

R152 contains 10 rows:

- 4 source proposition rows
- all 6 pairwise proposition comparisons

The source-family tags YUANHAI and SANMING are descriptive labels derived from the R071 IDs.

They are not treated as verified school-lineage assertions.

## Pairwise comparison

The six comparisons are represented as:

- five PARTIAL_OVERLAP relations
- one INCOMPARABLE relation

Composition disposition is kept separate from proposition relation:

- PARALLEL_PRESERVATION_ONLY: 1
- CONDITIONAL_COMPLEMENT_CANDIDATE: 2
- OPERATIONAL_MAPPING_REQUIRED: 3

No pair emits a method winner.

## Five-year split boundary

The source says:

運行十載數，上下五年分

R071 does not authorize a direct mapping such as:

- first five years = stem only, second five years = branch only
- first five years = branch, second five years = stem

R152 therefore treats the five-year proposition as a temporal scaffold whose operational mapping remains unresolved.

The Sanming stem-period and branch-period formulations may be represented as conditional-complement candidates, but that is not an executable mapping.

## Lineage boundary

R095 requires lineage relation and proposition relation to remain separate.

Therefore R152 explicitly rejects:

- different source labels imply different schools
- same source-family label implies semantic equivalence
- source-family tags as authority weights

School-lineage divergence remains unestablished.

## Composition boundary

R139 and R140 prevent methodological coexistence from turning into:

- source-count voting
- majority voting
- numeric method priority
- array-order precedence
- automatic conflict tie-break
- one canonical Dayun method

R152 preserves unresolved mappings instead.

## Relation to R151

R151 established that Dayun meaning depends on natal context and that temporal overlay is distinct from permanent natal mutation.

R152 does not bypass that boundary.

Even if a future stem/branch methodology is selected, it still must operate inside natal-context temporal reasoning.

## Rejected collapses

R152 rejects:

- 50/50 stem-branch weighting
- 30/70 stem-branch weighting
- universal branch-over-stem precedence
- source label as canonical method
- source-family tag as school lineage
- source count / popularity / seniority as method winner
- array order / first match as method selection
- invented five-year stem/branch half assignment
- branch-period stem discard as universal stem ignoring
- stem-period branch participation as universal equal weighting
- numeric weighting to reconcile unresolved methods
- automatic canonical Dayun method selection
- methodology variance as InterpretationClaim authority

## Authority

R152 establishes:

- preservation of all four R071 source propositions
- explicit pairwise proposition comparison
- partial overlap and incomparability where supported by the represented semantics
- conditional-complement candidates without execution
- preservation of the five-year operational mapping gap
- preservation of methodology formulation variance

R152 does not establish:

- verified school-lineage divergence
- semantic equivalence
- universal stem/branch precedence
- numeric stem/branch weights
- five-year stem/branch operational assignment
- a method winner
- an executable Dayun weighting resolver
- engine admission
- InterpretationClaim emission
- preview promotion
- production promotion

R153 should next address annual stem/branch composition and Dayun–annual layering using R074 without importing a resolved R152 method where none exists.
