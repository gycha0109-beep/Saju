## Source verification

The governed R034/R039 source surface is 徐樂吾's commentary on 子平真詮, especially 論用神配氣候得失.

The source explicitly requires climate to be considered together with structural/support reasoning, while also preserving cases where the climate-required element is not the final structural use. The existing R039 seed corpus therefore remains the primary direct control.

A fresh verification on 2026-09-26 confirmed the same source surface still contains:
- winter metal/water cases where fire is climate-relevant but not automatically the final use;
- a strong winter-metal case using 傷官洩秀 while 丙火 remains a required climate condition;
- a weak winter-metal case retaining support as the structural use;
- configuration-sensitive exceptions where climate urgency changes what would otherwise be a structural taboo.

These are evidence for coexistence and context dependence, not a universal Tiaohou-over-structure precedence rule.
