## Direct source surfaces

### 淵海子平
`子平之法，大運看支，歲君看干`

This records an annual-stem emphasis tradition.

### 流年賦 commentary
`歲干如君，固應從重；歲支為輔，實則同功`

This explicitly keeps the annual branch operative even while the stem is emphasized.

### 再論歲運
The quoted tradition describes 太歲 as `重天干，未曾無地支` and evaluates battle/clash/combination using both stem and branch, including whether the stem is rooted or weakened by its branch.
