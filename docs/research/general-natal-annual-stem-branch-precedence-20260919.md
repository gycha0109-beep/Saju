## Direct source surfaces

### 淵海子平
Source: https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E6%B7%B5%E6%B5%B7%E5%AD%90%E5%B9%B3

`子平之法，大運看支，歲君看干`

This records an annual-stem emphasis tradition.

### 流年賦 commentary
Source: https://www.ncc.com.tw/fate/paleo/bg/bg_19.htm

`歲干如君，固應從重；歲支為輔，實則同功`

This explicitly keeps the annual branch operative even while the stem is emphasized.

### 再論歲運
Source: https://www.ncc.com.tw/fate/paleo/bg/bg_17.htm

The source directly preserves `太歲...重天干，未曾無地支` and separately explains `干支兩字相聯，以支為根`. These are retained as bounded direct excerpts rather than merged into a synthetic quotation.

### 流年賦 composition surface
Source: https://www.ncc.com.tw/fate/paleo/bg/bg_19.htm

`先觀歲與日干，或為利、或為害；次詳歲與大運，或相順、或相攻。問其有無會合，考其是否刑沖。`

This is retained as a direct quoted composition surface. The proposition `ANNUAL_STATE_COMPOSES_WITH_DAYUN_AND_NATAL` is a research abstraction from that surface, not a replacement quotation.
