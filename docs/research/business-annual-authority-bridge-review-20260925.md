The Business Natal registry conservatively requires all families participating in a multi-family specialist conclusion to co-occur in a visible-stem channel or in a branch channel.

That shared-channel gate is an evidence-specificity constraint.

- a Business Annual semantic source;
- a temporal authority transfer mechanism;
- a numeric dominance score;
- a strength classifier;
- proof that a Business Natal conclusion becomes valid for an Annual period.

Therefore:

```text

+ shared-channel gate
```
