## Direct-body review

### 1. Explicit sex-split Yukchin allocation

**printed p.84 / physical 94** directly presents separate tables titled `男命六親取用圖` and `女命六親取用圖`.

This is not merely an editorial label. The underlying kinship slots differ by native sex.

### 2. Author's own synthesis preserves male→Wealth/wife and female→Officer/husband

**printed p.144 / physical 154** is decisive because it is the author's own synthesis rather than only a classical quotation.

The thesis explains that Yukchin family relations are built by analogizing Yin/Yang to man/woman and combining them through the Five-Element generating/overcoming structure. It then handles cases where the Day Stem's Yin/Yang does not align with that analogy and explicitly states:

```text
陰 Day-Stem man → Wealth as wife
陽 Day-Stem woman → Officer as husband
```

The same page further generalizes that Wealth is wife for a man and the controlling element/Officer is husband for a woman.

This directly defeats native-sex-independent spouse-star admission.

### 3. Cross-relative reinterpretation is real but does not neutralize spouse selection

**printed pp.146-150 / physical 156-160** materially expand the method.

The thesis shows that one natal symbol can be read from the perspectives of different relatives, and that Yukchin meanings can be extended from kinship into actions, wealth, office, social activity, and institutions.

However the worked examples retain the same sex-conditioned kinship assignments. A female chart still uses Officer as husband; a male chart uses Officer as children. The cross-relative mechanism changes viewpoint and semantic role, not the source's spouse selector contract.

Therefore:

```text
cross-relative semantic expansion = YES
social / institutional expansion  = YES
role-neutral spouse replacement    = NO
```

### 4. Spouse-palace location is broader than spouse-star semantics

**printed p.167 / physical 177** labels the Day position as `자신과 배우자 宮`, which is a materially useful sex-common spouse-palace location layer.

But the classical rule quoted immediately below still identifies the Day Branch as wife/concubines. More importantly, later spouse-star rules remain explicitly wife/husband conditioned.

A sex-common location cannot be promoted into a sex-neutral star selector by inference.

### 5. Wife palace + wife star remain explicitly Wealth-based

**printed p.172 / physical 182** says that the `妻宮` is the Day Branch and the `妻星` is Wealth in the heavenly stems.

This is a wife-specific spouse-star rule, not a role-neutral partner rule.

### 6. Functional substitution case remains female/husband-specific

**printed pp.173-174 / physical 183-184** review a female chart with no exposed Officer.

The cited classical case calls Wealth husband because Fire leaks Wood and generates Earth. The author's own analysis then tightens the interpretation: the Earth branch contains hidden `辛金`, and that hidden Direct Officer is the husband. The author also connects that branch to the spouse palace through combination.

This is important evidence of case-specific functional reasoning, but it does not publish a general replacement selector. The author's final spouse anchor remains the female chart's Officer/husband relation.

Therefore:

```text
case-specific functional substitution = YES
role-neutral replacement selector      = NO
```

### 7. Male worked example again uses Wealth/wife and Officer/child

**printed pp.175-176 / physical 185-186** analyze a male chart.

The spouse palace and Earth/Wealth are read as the wife, while hidden Officer is read as the child. This directly preserves the sex-conditioned branch in a modern explanatory passage.

### 8. Conclusion and proposal preserve male-female marital construction

**printed pp.177-179 / physical 187-189** are decisive because they summarize the author's final position.

The conclusion states that the Six-Relative system constructs family relations from Yin/Yang male-female union, marriage, and children. It argues that Wealth and Officer are important because of theoretical balance rather than merely because of historical social hierarchy, and gives Direct Wealth as wife-or-wealth as an example of differentiated semantic application.

The proposal again states that Yin and Yang combine to establish marital relations and that Five-Element interactions then derive multiple family relations.

Thus the paper's modernization is a reinterpretation of meaning and application, not a publication of a role-neutral spouse-selector contract.

**printed p.180 / physical 190** continues with terminology and counseling scope but does not provide a native-sex-independent or partner-sex-independent spouse selector.
