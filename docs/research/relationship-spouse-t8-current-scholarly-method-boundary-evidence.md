### 3. Single-native spouse-relationship feature list

- a weak spouse star;
- spouse-star 空亡, 偏重, 混雜, 怨嗔, 白虎殺;

These are material body-level method passages on the author-hosted indexed surface. They are **not** permissions for the current engine to calculate semantic states that are absent from governed canonical authority.

### 4. Dyadic compatibility remains part of the method

The KCI abstract and indexed body describe contemporary compatibility as comparing both partners' complete year/month/day/hour charts.

The indexed text additionally discusses factors such as:

- balance of Yin-Yang/Five Elements and 財官印食 strength;
- 干合 / 六合;
- a partner supplying Five Elements needed or absent in the native chart;
- shared 用神;

Thus the source includes both:

1. single-native spouse-relationship observations; and
2. a two-chart compatibility procedure.

These must not be collapsed into one single-native Relationship T6 rule.
