## Checklist states

`BLOCKED | RESEARCH_READY | REVIEW_READY | SHADOW_READY | PROMOTION_ELIGIBLE`

`PROMOTION_ELIGIBLE` means the evidence package is eligible for an authority decision. It does **not** mutate lifecycle/status by itself.
