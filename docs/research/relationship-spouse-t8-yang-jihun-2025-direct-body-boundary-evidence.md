### Printed p.68 / physical PDF 76

The conclusion characterizes the female-fate theory of 『연해자평』 as evaluating auspiciousness and inauspiciousness with substantial emphasis on:

- husband relationship;
- children relationship; and
- married life.

It explains that this framework arose in a social context in which marriage and childbirth were treated as central to women's lives. The conclusion then identifies a modern applicability problem: a husband/children-centered framework is difficult to apply universally to unmarried women or to married women who do not want childbirth.

This is direct evidence of a modern applicability critique.

It is not, by itself, a new spouse-star selection rule.
