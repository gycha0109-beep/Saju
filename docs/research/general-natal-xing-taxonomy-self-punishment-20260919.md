The directional arrows record the explicit 三命通會 sequence. They do not imply a Production effect.

## Self-punishment

三命通會 explains 辰午酉亥 as 自刑 because each is the member left without another branch target inside the compared groupings.

The same source explicitly discusses repeated same-branch forms:

```text
辰見辰
午見午
酉見酉
亥見亥
```

R056 therefore records same-branch repetition as the bounded structural surface for self-punishment.

It does not authorize:
- a minimum duplicate-count rule beyond the observed repeated pair;
- hidden-stem self-punishment;
- a self-punishment severity score;
- automatic harmful interpretation.

## Taxonomy-label variance

Inherited sources/commentaries do not use every class-name explanation identically. Labels such as `無恩` and `恃勢` must therefore carry source-stratum metadata rather than becoming an unqualified canonical ontology.

The structural member relations are more stable than the explanatory labels.
