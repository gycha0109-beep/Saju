
## Temporal authority boundary

```text
Natal Authority
!= Annual Authority

```

No Natal rule becomes Annual authority merely because it uses the same Ten-God or branch-relation substrate.

No Annual decision in this review creates Monthly authority.

## Allowed conclusion

The current repository supports only these conclusions:

1. the General Annual candidate is technically representable and executable as research;
2. the temporal input layer can supply bounded Annual facts;
3. the candidate explicitly blocks deterministic-event and outcome overreach;
4. Annual-specific interpretation authority is not yet established.

The Bridge therefore does not rewrite the candidate. It returns the semantic authority gap to Research.

## Research return

Research must establish, with source-qualified evidence:

1. the admissible meaning and strength of Annual stem Ten-God theme semantics;
2. the admissible meaning and strength of Annual-to-natal branch-clash tension semantics, if that rule family is retained;
3. Annual-specific scope, qualifiers, exceptions, and counterexamples;
4. whether any Natal authority is reusable claim-by-claim, rather than inherited wholesale;
5. a clean separation between product-policy packaging and Saju semantic authority.

Research must preserve the existing prohibition on deterministic events, luck scores, wealth magnitude, health outcomes, relationship outcomes, and guaranteed timing unless a later governed authority review explicitly establishes a narrower supported claim.

## Later governance

Source closure is not enough for Engine admission.

After Annual source authority is established, later work must still separately establish:

- a content-addressed review surface for the exact admissible Annual subjects;
- real domain ReviewAttestations;
- independent ReviewerTrustGrants;
- governed provenance-quality review;
- governed lifecycle-promotion review;
- a new Bridge admission decision.

No gate cascades automatically into the next one.

## Prohibited shortcuts

The review explicitly forbids:

- Natal → Annual automatic authority inheritance;
- internal product policy → Saju semantic authority;
- executable code or fixture coverage → authority;
- Reading Profile coverage → authority;
- Annual → Monthly automatic authority expansion;
- deterministic future-event inference;
- luck-score or wealth-magnitude inference;
- health, relationship, or guaranteed-timing outcomes;
- fabricated review/trust authority;
- automatic Research lifecycle promotion;
- Engine, Preview, Official Reading, or Production promotion from this review.
