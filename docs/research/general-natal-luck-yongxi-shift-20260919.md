## State separation

### 1. NATAL_ROLE_BASIS
Yong/Xi are defined from the natal structure and its needs.

### 2. LUCK_SUPPORT_OR_OPPOSITION
A luck item can support, oppose, bind, drain, or otherwise interact with the natal role configuration.

### 3. NATAL_RESCUE_OR_BLOCKING
Other natal material can reverse the apparent effect of a nominally supportive/adverse luck item.

### 4. STRUCTURAL_COMPLETION_OR_CHANGE
Some luck interactions can complete or change the operative structure. This is not the same thing as recomputing a fresh Yongshen every period.
