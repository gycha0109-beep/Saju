Selected surfaces:

The later commentary gives two different directions:

```text
branch hidden stem -> heavenly stem = 透干
heavenly stem -> branch root        = 通根
```

and explicitly pairs them:

```text
干以通根為美，支以透出為貴
凡八字支中所藏，必須透干；天干所用，必須通根
```

The statements are attributed to 徐樂吾's later commentary, not silently to 沈孝瞻's base text.

