3. Day Branch is at spouse-star `절` or `묘` state.
4. Spouse star is weak.
5. Spouse star is associated with `공망·편중·혼잡·원진·백호살`.
6. Spouse star is weakened through `묘절`, lack of root, strong `설기`, or `파극`.
7. Many chart elements share the Day Master element, with a stronger male-chart statement.
8. For women, many `식상` or `식상` in the Day Branch is treated as adverse.
9. The spouse-palace element repeats in other stems/branches.

These are source observations only. The repository does not adopt their outcome claims.
