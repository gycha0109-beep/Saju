# MyeongHa Face Reading Methodology Review Governance v1

Status: active
Effective date: 2026-09-04
Authority scope: methodology review promotion (`research` -> `reviewed`)

## Project-owner decision

The MyeongHa project owner is the final methodology-review authority at the current stage of the project.

- External experts are not required under the current governance model.
- A methodology may move from `research` to `reviewed` only after an explicit project-owner approval for that specific promotion.
- The required approval count is exactly one: the project owner.
- This governance decision does not itself approve any existing methodology or proposed successor.
- A target-specific approval must identify the exact research methodology, proposed reviewed successor, and source snapshot being reviewed.

## Evidence policy

For a target-specific methodology review decision, admissible governance evidence is an explicit project-owner approval bound to the exact target methodology, proposed successor, and source snapshot. Source verification, repository authorship, pull-request merge state, PIE operational readiness, or a legacy `reviewed` scalar do not substitute for that explicit approval.

## Boundaries

This governance decision establishes who may approve methodology review promotion and how many approvals are required. It does not establish traditional semantic equivalence, metric binding, calibration, thresholds, criterion states, structured claims, or narrative authority.

Consensus percentage is not configured because the current model has a single designated approver rather than a multi-reviewer consensus rule.
